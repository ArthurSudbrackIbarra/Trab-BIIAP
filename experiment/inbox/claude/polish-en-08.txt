### en-071
Anthropogenic interference has long affected the Earth's surface, and its intensity has increased in recent times because of land-use changes, which contribute to greenhouse gas emissions, especially carbon dioxide (CO2). This study analyzes land-use transitions and the resulting CO2 emissions in a watershed. Land-use maps were produced for 2007, 2010, 2013, and 2016, and emissions from the transitions were estimated. The calculation of net CO2 emissions combined data on the observed transitions, past vegetation, and pedological information. All procedures were performed using geoprocessing and remote sensing techniques, yielding transition and CO2 emission matrices as well as spatialized information. The forest category showed the highest conversion to other land-use types, with a loss of 208.86 ha between 2010 and 2013. Over the nine-year period, carbon emissions exceeded carbon sequestration from the atmosphere. These findings highlight the need for management and planning to mitigate the impacts of intense land-use change in the studied watershed.

### en-072
Guava rootstocks can be propagated by minicuttings in an environment with high relative humidity, which requires an acclimatization period. Certain growth regulators favor acclimatization, and brassinosteroids have been used successfully in other fruit species. This study aimed to shorten the propagation time of the BRS Guaraçá hybrid by applying Biobras-16 (BB-16) during the acclimatization of seedlings obtained from minicuttings. The treatments consisted of five BB-16 concentrations (0, 0.1, 0.3, 0.5, and 1.0 mg L-1), applied in a randomized block design with a split-plot arrangement, four replications, and five seedlings per plot. The experiment was repeated in two seasons: winter and spring/summer. After 60 days of acclimatization, shoot and root growth indicators increased as a function of BB-16 application, particularly at concentrations between 0.3 and 0.6 mg L-1. Brassinosteroid can be used to stimulate the growth of BRS Guaraçá seedlings obtained from minicuttings, shortening production time and improving seedling quality.

### en-073
BACKGROUND: The introduction of anti-TNF agents was a landmark in the management of both Crohn's disease (CD) and ulcerative colitis (UC), offering improved efficacy and safety compared with conventional treatment. However, significant challenges remain in Latin America in facilitating access to biological agents for physicians and patients. OBJECTIVE: To summarize current evidence on the penetration of biological agents for CD and UC in Latin America. METHODS: Data were derived from a previous comprehensive systematic review that explored different characteristics of inflammatory bowel disease (IBD) in Latin America. Studies fully included in that review that provided detailed descriptions of the percentage of use of biological agents in different cohorts across Latin American and Caribbean countries were included, and descriptive findings were compiled to describe the penetration of these drugs for CD and UC in different patient cohorts from different countries. RESULTS: Of the 61 studies included in the original systematic review, only 19 reported the percentage of patients treated with biological agents. Anti-TNF use in CD varied from 1.51% in Mexico to 46.9% in Colombia, with most studies reporting anti-TNF use in approximately 20%-40% of CD patients. In contrast, the use of biologics was clearly lower in UC, varying from 0% in 2009 to 16.2% in 2018, according to two different Mexican studies. Only two studies described the penetration of anti-TNF agents in IBD overall: 13.4% in a Colombian study and 37.93% in a Brazilian study. No studies described the percentage of use of newer biological agents (vedolizumab and ustekinumab). CONCLUSION: Penetration of anti-TNF agents in Latin America is comparable to that in the rest of the world for CD but lower for UC. With the increasing incidence and prevalence of IBD, specific strategies are warranted to increase access to anti-TNF agents in UC and to newer biological agents overall.

### en-074
Different types of mathematical models have been used to predict the dynamic behavior of the novel coronavirus (COVID-19). Many of them involve the formulation and solution of inverse problems, which are generally addressed by treating the model, the vector of design variables, and the system parameters as deterministic values. In this contribution, we evaluate a methodology based on a double-loop iteration process, designed to assess the influence of uncertainties on inverse problems. The inner optimization loop finds the solution associated with the highest probability value, while the outer loop is the regular optimization loop used to determine the vector of design variables. For this task, we use an inverse reliability approach and the Differential Evolution algorithm. For illustration purposes, the proposed methodology is applied to estimate the parameters of the SIRD (Susceptible-Infectious-Recovered-Dead) model describing the dynamic behavior of the COVID-19 pandemic, using real data from the epidemic in China and considering uncertainties in the basic reproduction number (R0). The results demonstrate, as expected, that an increase in reliability implies an increase in the objective function value.

### en-075
The expansion of the security agenda underlies the emergence of new theoretical concepts in the field of international security studies. One example is the concept of securitisation, developed by the Copenhagen School, which makes it possible to examine both new threats to national security and the policies through which states seek to address them. Based on this concept, this article argues that drug trafficking was securitised by the Brazilian government in the period 2011-2016. From 2016, with the issue of Decree no. 8903, the matter returned to the stage of 'politicisation', as understood by the Copenhagen School. The decree therefore marked a process of desecuritisation of the issue in Brazil, since it revoked the Strategic Border Plan, resulting in the loss of the temporary and emergency nature of the 'Ágata' operations. This article analyses the development of Brazilian legislation on this matter since 1976 and conducts a content analysis of the narrative on securitisation for the period 2011 to 2016. In addition, it examines the guidelines and nature of the Brazilian government's public policies aimed at combating drug trafficking.

### en-076
Spatial and temporal variations in streams cause large fluctuations in resource availability, thereby affecting fish diets. We evaluated the effects of hydrological period and stream order within periods on the diet of the flag tetra Hyphessobrycon heterorhabdus. We analyzed 160 stomachs from eight streams ranging from 1st to 3rd order, sampled in the dry and flood periods. The sampled streams belonged to a well-preserved area in the Eastern Amazon. The flag tetra is omnivorous, with a tendency toward insectivory. During the dry period, the species consumed more autochthonous than allochthonous items. Fish consumed more allochthonous items in 1st- and 2nd-order streams in the dry period and in 1st- and 3rd-order streams in the flood period. These results reflect the interactions between temporal and longitudinal factors on resource availability and their influence on fish diet. This pattern probably depends on the extensive riparian vegetation, which serves as a direct and indirect food source for stream fish.

### en-077
This work investigates the influence of the number of floors on the instability parameter limit α1 of reinforced concrete frame-braced buildings. It follows earlier work in this field in which the same question was investigated for wall- and core-braced buildings. First, we show how ABNT NBR 6118:2014 (the Brazilian code for the design of concrete structures) defines when a second-order analysis is required. Topics concerning the consideration of physical nonlinearity and the lateral deflection components of frames are also presented. An analytical study then leads to the derivation of a method for determining the limit α1 as a function of the number of floors and the ratio between bending and shear stiffness. Finally, examples are presented, and their results are used to check the accuracy of the method.

### en-078
This paper presents an optimal thruster configuration for the attitude control subsystem of a spacecraft. The optimal configuration is designed according to the minimum number of thrusters required to satisfy a desired reliability at a specific redundancy level. A genetic algorithm is employed for the optimization process, and the feasibility of the results is evaluated using algebraic and geometric methods. Among the feasible configurations with the minimum number of required thrusters, the proposed configuration, which was selected as optimal, offers maximum reliability and minimum fuel consumption. In addition to feasibility, the attitude control performance of several configurations is examined through simulation, and the results confirm that the proposed configuration performs satisfactorily. Notably, the configuration with the maximum number of thrusters, which is the conventional configuration in which each thruster belongs to only one control channel, consumes less fuel than the optimal configuration. However, the total mass of the optimal configuration is lower than that of the conventional configuration because it uses fewer thrusters.

### en-079
Neben seinen ethnologischen und linguistischen Studien zu indigenen Kulturen der Amazonasregion beschäftigte sich der deutsche Brasilienforscher Theodor Koch-Grünberg auch mit indigenen Mythen und hatte klare Vorstellungen davon, wie diese mündlich überlieferten Erzählungen dokumentiert und übersetzt werden sollten. Modellcharakter hatte für ihn die interlineare Übersetzung des brasilianischen Historikers João Capistrano de Abreu von Texten aus dem Kashinawa, einer Panosprache, die noch heute im brasilianisch-peruanischen Grenzgebiet von Mitgliedern der ethnischen Gruppe gesprochen wird. Capistranos Textsammlung Rã-txa hu-ni ku-ĩ wurde 1914 in Brasilien veröffentlicht. Bereits 1920 erfolgte die Publikation der Indianermärchen aus Südamerika durch Koch-Grünberg, wobei von den 117 ins Deutsche übertragenen Geschichten 13 aus Capistranos Werk stammten. In diesem Beitrag soll anhand ausgewählter Textbeispiele die Übersetzung Koch-Grünbergs aus dem Kashinawa ins Deutsche diskutiert werden. Grundlage war dabei die transkribierte Textsammlung in Originalsprache mit einer Wort-für-Wort-Übersetzung ins Portugiesische sowie einem angehängten Glossar und einem Grammatiksketch. In Koch-Grünbergs eigenen Aufzeichnungen wird zusätzlich deutlich, dass es sich um einen multiplen Übersetzungsprozess handelt: von einem mündlich präsentierten narrativen Diskurs mit performativen Elementen in Schriftsprache, von einer indigenen Sprache Südamerikas in eine typologisch weit entfernte europäische und von einer vollkommen unbekannten Kultur in eine für den brasilianischen wie den deutschen Leser nachvollziehbare.

### en-080
This study contributes to the literature on the influence of macroeconomic factors on accounting information quality by analyzing firms' earnings management strategies and identifying distinct discretionary behaviors across economic cycles: (1) different levels of accrual-based earnings management between phases of the business cycle, and (2) the trade-off between accrual-based and real earnings management. The results indicate that users should analyze reported accounting information with greater caution, especially in periods of large economic oscillations, when managers may increase or reduce opportunistic behavior. The research population comprised non-financial companies with shares traded on the São Paulo Stock, Commodities, and Futures Exchange (BM&FBovespa). The sample comprised 247 firms per year over the period from 2000 to 2015, totaling 2,501 observations. Business cycle phases were used as a proxy for the economic environment and were based on Schumpeter (1939), who divides a business cycle into four distinct phases: expansion, recession, contraction, and recovery. Discretionary accruals were estimated using the models of Pae (2005) and Paulo (2007). Real earnings management was estimated following Roychowdhury (2006), using only the abnormal behavior of production costs and operational decisions. The results show that earnings management strategies, whether based on accruals or real manipulation, as well as the choice between them, are affected by the economic environment. The evidence suggests that managers exhibit different opportunistic behavior in each phase of the business cycle. Specifically, they increase the level of discretionary accruals in contractionary phases and reduce it during recoveries, while they manage earnings downwards via real manipulation in recessions and contractions.
