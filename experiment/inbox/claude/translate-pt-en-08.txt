### tr-071
This article aims to analyze the content and material aspects of the primer "Viver é lutar" ("To Live Is to Struggle"), published in 1963, which was part of the didactic collection of the same name developed by the Movimento de Educação de Base (MEB, Grassroots Education Movement) as support for adult literacy classes in the context of Brazilian radio schools in the early 1960s, considering the political context of the Brazilian dictatorial regime (1964-1985) and the repression of social movements dedicated to adult education. "O Conjunto Didático 'Viver é lutar'", reports produced by the MEB, and letters written by monitors who took part in the movement were also used as documentary sources. The didactic material shows an effort to bring in elements of the popular culture of the sertão as a didactic-pedagogical strategy to generate identification with the peasants served by the movement, while also taking into account the different intellectual and ideological tendencies in dispute within the Catholic Church that were mobilized in the construction of the material, related to its political, pedagogical, and religious conceptions, which resulted in a distinctive model of peasant education. Finally, we address the process of restructuring the MEB in the context of the dictatorial regime which, according to recent historiography, took place through processes of accommodation to the regime, the consequence of which was the reorganization of its pedagogical principles and the removal of militants from the more radical wings of the movement.

### tr-072
Remote sensing can be used to estimate meteorological data. Using images of atmospheric variables obtained by the AIRS (Atmospheric Infrared Sounder) sensor aboard the Aqua satellite, this study aimed to estimate the daily maximum, minimum, and mean air temperatures in the Federal District region – Brazil. Simple and multiple linear regression models were generated, with surface temperature, relative humidity, and atmospheric water vapor data from the AIRS sensor as explanatory variables, obtained at a twelve-hour temporal scale (daytime and nighttime) during the period from January 2012 to December 2016. Observed air temperature values from two meteorological stations in the studied region were used as dependent variables. The fits showed R² = 0.62 and RMSE = 1.64 °C for maximum temperature data, R² = 0.59 and RMSE = 1.50 °C for minimum temperature data, and R² = 0.76 and RMSE = 1.45 °C for mean temperature data. It is concluded that AIRS sensor data are suitable for monitoring air temperature in the Federal District region.

### tr-073
Yeasts have shown good results in dye biodegradation, making them an environmentally safe and lower-cost alternative for the treatment of effluents containing industrial dyes. Thus, the objective of this work was to study the decolorization of the azo dye Reactive Black 5 (RB5) by the yeast Pichia kudriavzevii SD5. To optimize the decolorization parameters for Reactive Black 5, two experimental designs of the 2³ Central Composite Rotatable Design (CCRD) type were carried out, each design consisting of 17 trials, at 150 rpm for 24 h. The variables studied were pH, temperature, and dye concentration, and the response was the percentage of decolorization. Toxicity tests of the supernatant were also performed after 16 h and 24 h of cultivation, using lettuce seeds (Lactuca sativa) and the microcrustacean Artemia salina. The results showed that P. kudriavzevii SD5 is a yeast tolerant to various types of stress, since it was able to degrade high concentrations of the dye RB5 at 45 °C. However, only temperature had a statistically significant influence (p < 0.05) on the decolorization of RB5. The toxicity bioassays showed that toxicity decreased after 24 h of cultivation, and the absorbance profile of the supernatant pointed to a degradative decolorization mechanism.

### tr-075
Hydroxyapatite (HAp) is present in the human body in bone and dental tissues, where HAp accounts for 30 to 70% of the total mass of each tissue, and it can be applied as a coating for prostheses or even as a replacement for small bone parts. Thus, the objective was to synthesize HAp by precipitation, incorporate chlorhexidine (CHX), and evaluate its microbial properties. Characterization was performed by XRD, FTIR, TG/DTG, TEM, and SEM. CHX was incorporated into HAp by adsorption, varying the amount incorporated. The diffractogram showed peaks at 25.9°, 31.5°, 32.2°, and 32.7° (2θ), corresponding to the (002), (211), (112), and (300) planes of HAp. The FTIR spectrum showed a band at 1050 cm-1 corresponding to the deformation of phosphate groups and CH2 group deformations at 1485 and 1445 cm-1 of chlorhexidine, which showed porous and cylindrical morphology. The biomaterial showed excellent antimicrobial properties against Streptococcus mutans, Staphylococcus aureus, and Escherichia coli.

### tr-076
More than half of the new HIV infections in 2018 occurred among key populations, including gay men and other men who have sex with men (MSM). Stigma can have a range of behavioral consequences, negatively affecting individuals' lives, and can translate into barriers to accessing public health services. The objective of this article is to analyze the main barriers to access to HIV testing at a Counseling and Testing Center in Curitiba and the linkage of gay men and other MSM to the health service for early treatment. Primary data were collected following a qualitative method, triangulating the techniques of field observation, document analysis, and interviews. The results reveal a lack of trained professionals, difficulty in being welcomed at health services, acceptance of one's sexuality, fear of the result, and lack of information as the main barriers to testing. Difficulty in accepting the diagnosis, prejudice, and discrimination were some of the barriers found to starting treatment. It can be concluded that expanding access to guarantee the rights of those who most struggle to enter the health services of the Unified Health System (SUS) remains a major challenge.

### tr-077
The objective of the study was to evaluate the floristic and structural composition of a stretch of secondary forest in the municipality of Dona Francisca, RS, Brazil. Fifty permanent plots measuring 10 x 20 m were installed systematically, spaced 10 m apart and 40 m between strips, totaling 1 ha. All individuals with a CBH (circumference at breast height) ≥ 15.7 cm were sampled and phytosociological estimators were calculated. A species accumulation curve was constructed to verify sampling sufficiency. To calculate diversity and dominance, the Shannon Index (H') and Pielou's Evenness (J') were used. For potential richness, the nonparametric Jackknife estimators were used. Floristic and structural composition was evaluated using Detrended Correspondence Analysis (DCA). The diameter and hypsometric distributions of the tree community were also verified, as well as the distribution in DBH classes for species with an importance value above 5%. Statistical analyses were performed in the R program. A total of 65 tree and shrub species with 2,045 individuals, distributed in 31 botanical families, were identified. The species with an importance value above 5% were Casearia sylvestris (14.04%), Nectandra lanceolata (12.86%), Cupania vernalis (9.57%), Guarea macrophylla (7.29%), and Ocotea puberula (5.07%). The values for diversity and dominance were, respectively, 2.91 nats.ind-1 and 0.69, indicating that the area has low diversity and dominance by a few species. The DCA shows that the plots are homogeneously distributed across the forest stretch. The diameter distribution was as expected for the tree community, with a tendency toward a reverse J shape, and most trees had heights concentrated in the intermediate stratum of the forest. Of the five species analyzed, only Nectandra lanceolata and Ocotea puberula did not show a negative exponential curve. A predominance of individuals in the first DBH and height classes was observed, which characterizes the resilience of the area after disturbance. It also indicates the fluctuations and stability of the species, in the ecological succession, of a forest at an intermediate stage of regeneration.

### tr-078
Observations were made on the reproductive biology of a recently described species of Rapateaceae (Saxofridericia brasiliensis) in an area of periodically flooded Campina Fechada. During the flowering period, the inflorescences were bagged in order to identify whether the species sets fruit through spontaneous self-pollination. The species has trichomes at the base of the petals that stained with Neutral Red, and its main pollinators are females of nine species of Euglossa, which collect pollen by vibrating the anthers. However, further studies are needed to fully understand the reproductive strategies of the family, given reports of hummingbird pollination in the tribe Schoenocephalieae owing to the presence of nectaries.

### tr-079
This article presents the results of a study on hybrid linguistic norms in New York Times journalistic articles translated into Portuguese and published on the UOL website. According to Faraco (2008) and Bagno (2012), there is a difference between the standard norm (norma padrão) and the cultured norm (norma culta): the former is the set of rules that prescribe linguistic forms to be followed according to an idealized model of language, but which is difficult for most users to follow; the latter is the set of linguistic forms that are actually used by many writers and are therefore more intuitively accessible, although not accepted by the conservative grammatical tradition represented by the standard norm, commonly disseminated in grammar compendiums and style manuals. The aim is to assess whether it is possible to state that journalistic texts translated from English, similarly to what has already been proven to occur with journalistic texts originally written in Portuguese, are equally permeable to linguistic forms not sanctioned by the standard norm.

### tr-080
This essay adopts Foucauldian thought as a basis for proposing a theoretical step back around the notion of the "right to look" as a claim to a position in the struggles over "how to see." After contextualizing the debate, I review two specific texts that engage with Foucault: Can the Subaltern Speak? by Gayatri Spivak, and Necropolitics by Mbembe. From the first, I infer the general idea that the right to look is the opposite of the right to "see without being seen." From the second, I deduce a function of opacity and of management of invisibility that necropolitics exerts in conjunction with biopolitics. Finally, I argue that, if all "seeing" depends on a "not seeing," the right to look claims a position that is always yet to be constructed.
