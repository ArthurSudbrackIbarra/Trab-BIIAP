### en-061
Nuclear techniques based on the attenuation of gamma radiation are used in industry to measure flow rate, determine fluid density, predict inorganic scale in oil pipelines, and evaluate industrial mixers, among other applications. Applying these techniques requires studying key parameters of the radioactive source and radiation detectors that form part of the measurement geometry, such as detection efficiency and solid angle. The aim of this study was to calculate the solid angle and the effective solid angle subtended by a NaI(Tl) detector. The effective solid angle accounts for attenuation in the medium between the source and the detector, as well as other effects of radiation interaction with matter. Mathematical models were developed using the MCNP6 code to evaluate the proposed measurement geometry. The source was placed at different positions relative to the detector to evaluate the frontal and lateral solid angle contributions, an important parameter for obtaining the intrinsic efficiency response function. The simulated model consisted of a NaI(Tl) scintillator detector and two isotropic point sources (241Am and 137Cs). For the geometry used in this study, the difference between the solid angle and the effective solid angle reached 20.17% for 241Am and 2.58% for 137Cs, indicating that the effective solid angle should be considered in these calculations.

### en-062
This paper analyzes the role of industrial policy in promoting technological upgrading in Brazil. To this end, we focus on the main characteristics of the Brazilian national innovation system and the role of its institutions. We examine whether the industrial policy implemented between 2003 and 2014 was able to change agents' conventions towards more vigorous strategies linked to technological upgrading and catch-up. In addition, we analyze the Brazilian mining industry, a sector in which Brazil has developed relevant competitive advantages in international markets and in which domestic agents have built important operational capabilities. Our main results, based on the unsuccessful policy experiences between 2002 and 2014, show the considerable difficulties faced by industrial policy in establishing mechanisms to drive technological catch-up. The specific experience of the Brazilian mining industry confirms these general findings, since domestic mining companies were unable to create new technological capabilities, even during the commodity price boom.

### en-063
This article analyzes temporality in Leon Kirchner's Interlude I for solo piano in light of Jonathan Kramer's book The Time of Music. The first section introduces the composer, covering his biography, influences, and compositional style. The second section presents Kramer's book and his concepts of linear and nonlinear time in music. An analysis of aspects of the Interlude's linear time, as defined by Kramer, follows, including linearity manifested through developing variation, stepwise motion, tonal references, tempo changes, flexion count changes, and transpositions. The next section examines manifestations of nonlinear time in the Interlude, such as balance and recapitulation, and discusses the implications of Kramer's concept of nonlinear moment time. The conclusion summarizes the interactions between linearity and nonlinearity in the Interlude.

### en-064
This work aims to verify the influence of the characteristic compressive cylinder strength (fck), section geometry, and eccentric axial load on the strength of square, cross-shaped, T-shaped, and L-shaped reinforced concrete sections under oblique composite flexion. A computational algorithm was developed to calculate the bending strength interaction diagrams of the sections, considering the idealized parabola-rectangle stress-strain relationships of NBR 6118 for concretes with fck from 20 to 90 MPa. The results show that the influence of fck is stronger at higher axial loads and that the shape of the failure surface in the interaction diagrams depends directly on fck and on the distribution of rebars in the section. Furthermore, under lower compressive axial loads, higher oblique composite flexion strengths are achieved when more reinforcement area is located in tension regions; as compression increases, however, the presence of reinforcement and larger concrete areas in compression zones provide higher bending moment strengths.

### en-065
Purpose: This study presents a guide to using grounded theory methods for exploring organizational phenomena in the new online era. Design/methodology/approach: A reflexive account is adopted of how one can build upon the foundations of traditional offline grounded theory to conduct grounded theorizing with online-based data. Findings: Guidelines are presented for conducting grounded theory in online contexts, including crafting research questions, gathering online data, and using consolidated methods for analyzing online data. The study shows the present and future challenges that the new online era poses for grounded theorizing, as well as helpful lessons from traditional offline grounded theory for mitigating them. Research limitations/implications: The implications are useful for established qualitative organizational scholars who have yet to catch up in the boundary-spanning process of using digital data sources in grounded theory. They are equally useful for newcomers to qualitative grounded theory, guiding them on where and how to start these challenging research endeavors in the new online era. Originality/value: Scant attention has been given to applications of grounded theory in the new online era. The differences between online and offline settings have not been clearly defined to date, and no guidelines exist for how qualitative grounded theorists can take advantage of online data to build theory about new organizational phenomena emerging in the online era.

### en-066
A new species of Parotocinclus, apparently endemic to the rio Jacuípe basin, Bahia State, Brazil, is described. The new species is distinguished from its congeners, except P. bidentatus, P. cabessadecuia, P. dani, P. halbothi, P. muriaensis, P. pentakelis, P. seridoensis, and P. spilurus, by the presence of a reduced adipose fin. It differs from these species by the absence of unicuspid accessory teeth, an abdomen with broad naked areas between the lateral and medial patches of plates, and a snout tip completely covered by plates and odontodes. The adipose fin of the new species is restricted to the adipose-fin spine, lacking the adipose-fin membrane, a reduction pattern previously described for P. halbothi, a congener from rivers of the Amazonas and Marowijne basins.

### en-067
This study aimed to assess the effect of different spray volumes and fungicide rates on the control of Asian soybean rust. Experiments were conducted in the field and in the laboratory between 2014 and 2016. In the field, fungicide rates (45 + 52.5, 60 + 70, and 75 + 87.5 g a.i. ha-1 for the mixture trifloxystrobin + prothioconazole, and 45 + 22.5, 60 + 30, and 75 + 37.5 g a.i. ha-1 for azoxystrobin + benzovindiflupyr) and spray volumes (100, 150, and 200 L ha-1) were varied for application on soybean plants. A further experiment was conducted in the laboratory using the recommended rates of trifloxystrobin + prothioconazole (60 + 70 g a.i. ha-1) and azoxystrobin + benzovindiflupyr (60 + 30 g a.i. ha-1). The spray volume of 200 L ha-1 resulted in the best disease control only at the third of the three applications carried out in the field (control between 68.4% and 70.1%). However, grain yield did not differ statistically among the tested spray volumes, either with the recommended fungicide rates (3422, 3526, and 3601 kg ha-1) or with higher rates (3529, 3586, and 3550 kg ha-1). Reducing the fungicide rate directly decreased rust control in both experiments (by 20 to 39% in the field and by up to 71.4% in the laboratory).

### en-068
The reproduction of real estate capital through tourist facilities and secondary residences is one of the main modes of production in the coastal region, at times causing conflicts with the interests of remnant traditional populations. This article analyzes the granting of the Term of Authorization for Sustainable Use (TAUS), a land policy instrument for ownership regularization developed on the basis of recognizing the right of traditional populations to housing and to the management of their socioeconomic practices, to the Caiçara Traditional Communities located along Praia de Castelhanos, in Ilhabela (SP). The study was descriptive and exploratory, complemented by secondary data and participant observation. Issuing the TAUS gives the Caiçara decision-making power and the possibility of participation; however, given its uniqueness in territories desired for tourism, it appears inconsistent, especially regarding the practical aspects of territorial management and the disputes between these communities and external stakeholders.

### en-069
Aim: To evaluate the synergistic impact of muscular temporomandibular disorder (TMD) and malocclusion on the quality of life, masticatory capacity, and emotional features of young adults. Methods: This cross-sectional study comprised four groups (n = 15 each): G1, individuals without TMD or malocclusion; G2, with TMD and malocclusion; G3, with TMD and without malocclusion; and G4, without TMD and with malocclusion. Muscular TMD was diagnosed using the RDC/TMD. Data included quality of life (OHIP-14), masticatory capacity (X50), emotional stress (PSS-14), depression (MDI), pain intensity, and salivary cortisol. Comparative statistical analysis included one-way ANOVA with Tukey's post hoc test (X50, stress, and cortisol) and GENMOD followed by the Wald test (OHIP-14 and pain data). Fisher's and Pearson's association analyses were also performed. Results: Comorbidity of muscular TMD and malocclusion led to significantly lower masticatory capacity (p<0.05). Groups with TMD, regardless of occlusal condition, had considerably lower OHIP-14 scores and higher stress levels, both self-perceived and hormonal (p<0.05). No statistically significant difference in depression was found among groups. A significant positive correlation was observed among quality of life, stress, and pain perception. Conclusion: Muscular TMD overlapping with malocclusion potentiates their negative effect on masticatory capacity. Moreover, the detrimental effect of the comorbidity is variable: TMD has a greater negative impact on quality of life and stress, whereas malocclusion has a greater impact on mastication.

### en-070
Although inherent to the process of hegemonic globalization, English can become a language of denunciation and interaction among human rights defenders in the global South. Bearing this in mind, and based on intercultural dialogue with these voices from the South (SANTOS, 2010), we put forward a decolonial English language teaching curriculum designed to meet the needs and demands of these defenders. We used anonymous questionnaires in national and international contexts, whose 159 responses enabled a deeper understanding of defenders' identities, belongings, activisms, and linguistic engagements. First, we adopted a listening posture guided by Boaventura de Sousa Santos's intercultural imperatives (2010; 1997). In the course of this dialogical process, we then propose a curriculum organized into sections of themes, literacies, form-functions, and genres that help defenders project their agendas of activism and resistance in the international arena. Thus, mastering English in this context can reduce death threats against defenders by making their actions more visible and widely known.
