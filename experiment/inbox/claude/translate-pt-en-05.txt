### tr-041
The aim of this paper is to highlight the possible role of student exchange agencies as facilitators of immigration. In order to understand the consumption behavior and the factors that motivate Brazilians to immigrate to Australia, the researchers conducted a survey with 608 consumers, affiliated with 10 Facebook groups representing communities of Brazilians in Australia. Using a logistic regression model, the researchers defined the dependent variable of the study as the permanent immigrant, assigned the value 0 (zero) if respondents did not intend to live in Australia permanently, or 1 (one) otherwise. The independent variables, representing factors present at the time of their arrival in the country that may affect the decision to immigrate permanently, were gender, age at arrival, future intention to start a business, student visa, and educational level. The results point to statistically significant factors related to immigration to Australia, based on the respondents' sociodemographic profiles. In-depth interviews were also conducted with a travel agent and Brazilian residents. As practical implications, the paper highlights consumption behavior, motivations for immigration, and the factors that lead people to hire student exchange agencies.

### tr-042
Although Araliaceae has many species with leaves bearing toothed margins, the anatomical structure of these projections has never been investigated. Thus, this study aimed to characterize the anatomy of the leaf teeth of Hydrocotyle asterias Cham. & Schltdl., a representative of this family. Fragments of the leaf blade with marginal teeth were processed according to standard techniques for light microscopy (clearing, paradermal sections, and histochemical tests) and scanning electron microscopy. The teeth of H. asterias consist of hydathodes, whose size, shape, and spacing between teeth vary across the different stages of leaf development. They have a uniseriate epidermis composed of isodiametric cells with slightly thickened walls, a thin cuticle, and stomata concentrated in the apical region. The vascularization consists of xylem that extends up to the epithem. This study presents the first information on the anatomical structure of leaf teeth in the family Araliaceae and expands knowledge about hydathodes located on leaf teeth.

### tr-043
The present study highlights the trajectories of 19 Brazilian entrepreneurs of startups established in Canada. Data were collected through semi-structured interviews with the respondents. Given that there are few studies on Brazilian entrepreneurs in Canada, we sought to determine the barriers and main difficulties they face, as well as the opportunities and success strategies they have pursued in the country. The paper begins with a discussion of immigrant entrepreneurship and startup creation. It then addresses the migratory movement of Brazilians to Canada, covering Brazilian businesses in general and startups, also focusing on the definition of startups and their profile in Brazil, in addition to the Canadian startup ecosystem. The theoretical contribution of this paper is to compare the collected data with theories of immigrant entrepreneurship, effectuation theory applied to startups, and the Uppsala model, aiming to theoretically list the elements observed in the group studied. At the end of the paper, a diagram is presented that illustrates the immigrant entrepreneur's journey in the process of designing and validating their business model, and the conclusions may contribute to entrepreneurship theories by showing specific aspects of the Canadian startup context. As for practical implications, the study can provide data for possible future policies aimed at attracting more qualified and skilled Brazilian entrepreneurs to the Canadian technology ecosystem.

### tr-044
The objective of this study was to estimate the potential for soil loss using the Universal Soil Loss Equation and to identify the factors that control the erosive process in a hillslope watershed. The rainfall erosivity factor (R) was calculated from normal precipitation data, thus obtaining monthly erosion indices. The soil erodibility factor (K) was obtained from soil sampling carried out on a grid of 340 points, whose values were interpolated by the ordinary kriging method. The topographic factor (LS) was generated from the Digital Elevation Model (DEM), while the land use and management and conservation practice factors (CP) were obtained through field observations and tabulated values. The highest loss rates cover 27% of the area and are concentrated in locations with steeper slopes where shallow soils predominate, such as Lithic Neosols and Regolithic Neosols. The LS factor determined the magnitude of the erosive process, and the CP factor showed the strongest relationship with the control of soil losses. The results show the importance of the soil cover factor, indicating that vegetation maintenance measures and conservation practices should be adopted and considered by environmental managers in hillslope regions with a predominance of shallow soils associated with steep relief.

### tr-045
The present study presents an application of the WRF mesoscale model to the simulation of an intense and persistent wind episode that occurs with irregular frequency at the Guapé reservoir, in the state of Minas Gerais, Brazil. This event alters the routine of the population in the region, causing, for example, the vessel that makes the crossing between the cities of Guapé and Capitólio to suspend its service. During one of these episodes, which occurred on September 22, 2016, measurements were made using a Zephir ZP300 LIDAR and an anemometric tower, and these data were subsequently used to validate the simulations. To evaluate the results, simulated and observed pressure, temperature, humidity, and wind speed were compared. The analysis of the simulated vertical velocity profiles suggests the presence of a low-level jet, located at around 500 m above the surface, generated by a post-frontal temperature gradient, whose lower portion would have been accelerated by the terrain and by the variation in land-water surface roughness. Further studies are currently under way so that some type of preventive action can be suggested to the population and local authorities.

### tr-046
This paper analyzes the multiple dimensions of the work of street-level bureaucrats in the Brazilian context of public policy implementation. The aim of this essay is to contribute to a better understanding of the topic through an expanded analytical approach. To this end, a literature search was carried out in academic digital databases, which made it possible to identify and select national and international works according to methodologically defined criteria. The publications obtained were analyzed and organized according to the following conceptual categories: discretion, structures, individual action, and relationships. From the tension between rules and discretion in public administration, the analysis and interpretation of these texts showed: a) the need to compare, separately and jointly, three different perspectives on the work of street-level bureaucrats (the structural, the individual action, and the relational perspectives); b) the need to understand how these perspectives are articulated in the implementation process itself. Following this literature review, hypotheses were formulated for an expanded analysis of the work of implementing bureaucrats, outlining directions for future research on the topic. This essay contributes to the debate on the subject with a novel theoretical construct that seeks to integrate the three different analytical perspectives presented in a dialectical synthesis of the central concepts of the literature on public policy implementation by street-level bureaucrats.

### tr-047
Bacterial pustule, caused by the bacterium Xanthomonas axonopodis pv. glycines, is considered one of the most serious bacterial diseases in soybean. The objective of this work was to develop and validate a diagrammatic scale for assessing the severity of bacterial pustule in soybean. To develop the scale, soybean leaflets with different levels of severity were collected, thereby determining the maximum and minimum values of disease amount, and the intermediate severity levels of the scale were established according to the Weber-Fechner law of visual stimulus. The values proposed for the scale were: 1.4%; 3%; 6%; 9%; 20%, and 29.5%. To validate the scale, eight raters with no experience in assessing the disease evaluated 39 photographs with different severity levels, with and without the aid of the scale. The severity estimates given by the raters were compared with the actual severity values (determined with Assess 2.0 software from digital images of leaves with symptoms) and subjected to simple linear regressions to evaluate the raters' accuracy. The raters' precision was determined through the coefficients of determination and the variance of absolute errors. Estimates made using the proposed diagrammatic scale resulted in a significant increase in the coefficient of determination and a lower variance of absolute errors, providing greater precision and accuracy of the assessments, and the scale can be adopted for assessing the severity of bacterial pustule in soybean.

### tr-048

### tr-049
One of the alternatives for obtaining potential species for the revegetation of degraded areas is to use those that develop from the topsoil seed bank, and in this context, grasses are the most suitable because they have characteristics associated with soil stabilization. This study aimed to evaluate the native species that developed from a ferruginous topsoil seed bank and to select the grasses with the highest importance value index (IVI), considering them potential species for use in revegetation programs. The topsoil was deposited at Gerdau's Research and Innovation Unit in Ferruginous Rupestrian Fields (Campos Rupestres Ferruginosos), Ouro Branco, MG, Brazil, and after plant germination and establishment, phytosociological studies were carried out. Eragrostis polytricha Nees showed the highest values not only of IVI, but also of Relative Frequency and Relative Cover, and additionally showed the second highest Relative Density value. Therefore, we recommend E. polytricha for use in revegetation programs.

### tr-050
This study aimed to evaluate the effect of a Pinus taeda stand on the floristic composition and structure of the seed bank of a remnant of Araucaria Forest (Mixed Ombrophilous Forest, MOF). To this end, 160 seed bank samples were collected, 80 from a MOF remnant and 80 from a P. taeda stand. The samples were placed to germinate in two separate greenhouses, under 35% and 75% shading levels. A total of 11,669 woody and herbaceous individuals were counted, distributed among 31 botanical families and 84 species. The families Asteraceae and Poaceae were the most representative in herbaceous species richness. Among the woody species, Solanaceae and Asteraceae were the most representative in both areas. The species P. taeda was recorded only in the seed bank of the stand itself. The seed bank of the pine stand was characterized mainly by low woody species richness, which demonstrates its influence on the seed stock. On the other hand, the evaluated MOF remnant does not appear to be influenced by the proximity of the pine stand in terms of its seed bank.
