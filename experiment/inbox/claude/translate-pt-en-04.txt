### tr-031
Weather systems that cause storms accompanied by strong winds, such as cold fronts and extratropical and subtropical cyclones, often affect sea state conditions. As a consequence, the sea level may rise extraordinarily, causing coastal flooding. The years 2015 and 2016 were characterized by the occurrence of the positive phase of the El Niño–Southern Oscillation phenomenon, with very strong intensity. Southern Brazil showed greater atmospheric instability, with frequent passages of frontal systems and formation of extratropical cyclones, affecting sea conditions along the southern and southeastern coasts of Brazil. In view of the above, this study aimed to evaluate the atmospheric conditions during storm surge events on the southern and southeastern coasts of Brazil during the 2015/2016 El Niño, using information contained in the Bad Weather Warnings issued by the Brazilian Navy Hydrography Center. Using these warnings and ERA-Interim reanalysis data, distributions by maritime area, by month, and by season of the year were analyzed, and an analysis of synoptic fields plotted at the surface was carried out for the 12 most prominent events. As results, the autumn and winter months recorded the highest occurrences of storm surges in both areas during the 2015/2016 El Niño period. In general, four atmospheric patterns favorable to storm surge events were identified, resulting from the action of extratropical cyclones and the action of post-frontal high-pressure systems.

### tr-032
This article presents part of the research results of a dissertation that seeks to deepen reflections on librarians' practices and to identify their perceptions and meanings regarding professional practice and the university library in the context of cyberculture. The study was conducted in a university library in the medical field, and it presents the history and characteristics of this library and identifies, based on semi-structured interviews conducted with eight working or retired librarians, the meanings attributed to library practices. The narratives reveal a constant search for recognition and appreciation of the profession, as well as concern about maintaining users' attendance at the physical space of the library studied. Among the changes perceived in this practice, it is noted that the meaning of "mediator" is giving way to the meaning of "educator," and the stance of "waiting for the user" is giving way to that of "seeking out the user," which shows a concern about the future of the library and of the librarian's role. Meanings that may indicate strategies for maintaining the professional culture and remaining in the job market are also identified.

### tr-033
The evolution of the rural population and of some Portuguese autochthonous livestock breeds has shown worrying levels of decline, with environmental and ecological consequences, for land management and planning and for its sustainability, namely with regard to the socioeconomic dimension and territorial cohesion itself. In this study, based on interviews with breeders, companies, and associations dedicated to the breeding and protection of the Garrano equine breed, which is used for various purposes, we aim to identify the benefits of adopting a farming system that includes the Garrano, while also highlighting its main weaknesses. The approach will be developed considering the environmental, economic, and social dimensions of sustainability. The results show the importance of these animals, not only as living genetic heritage, but also in terms of protecting forests against fires, specifically in the control of combustible biomass. However, it will be necessary to define a concerted strategy that makes it possible to enhance their contribution to the economy of farms or companies, which should be reflected in increased well-being of the population, stabilization of the animal stock, and sustainability of the territory.

### tr-034
The State of Ceará stands out in the production of energy from wind because of its privileged geographic position for wind power generation. This work aims to present the environmental, social, economic, and technological impacts arising from the implementation and operation of wind power plants on the coast of Ceará through a comparative analysis between the Beberibe and Fleixeiras I Wind Farms. The main socio-environmental impacts detected were: reduction of fauna and flora, wear of access roads, noise production, alteration of the natural landscape, limitation of the right to "come and go," and cracks in houses. The economic impacts were most significant during the construction of the farms, gradually decreasing over the years. As for the technological aspects, it can be seen that the greater the height of the towers and the diameter of the rotor, the greater the amount of energy generated. This investigation was descriptive and exploratory in nature, describing the impacts using qualitative and quantitative methods. Directed studies were necessary to understand the impacts caused by wind energy, as well as to suggest alternatives for mitigating the worsening of climate change caused by the greenhouse effect on the planet.

### tr-035
The article examines the role of the Public Prosecutor's Office and the Court of Accounts of the state of Rio Grande do Sul within the national early childhood education policy, especially regarding the expansion of places in public schools. The axes of analysis, researched through document consultation and interviews with public prosecutors, auditors, and municipal managers, covered the characteristics of their activities, organizational changes, and the interaction among external control bodies. The results show that these bodies are not merely oversight agencies, as they directly influence early childhood education policies, as co-administrators (Hoffmann and Bentes 2008) or fixers (Bardach 1977; Sabatier and Mazmanian 1993; Weimer and Vining 2017) of their implementation by municipalities. It was also found that these accountability institutions have been assuming new responsibilities in the area of education since the 2000s, within an incremental development trajectory (Mahoney and Thelen 2009) marked by a variety of actions, including interinstitutional cooperation.

### tr-036
There are many challenges in minimizing the gaps between scientific evidence and better health outcomes. Knowledge translation (KT) is the movement that knowledge undergoes within an organizational context toward its effective application. The objective of this study was to analyze the occurrence of KT in the Executive Secretariat of Health Surveillance of Pernambuco (SEVS/PE), taking as its object ten evaluative studies on Health Surveillance programs, produced in the Master's Program in Health Evaluation of the Instituto de Medicina Integral Prof. Fernando Figueira, from 2011 to 2015. To this end, a semi-structured interview guide was used, involving seven managers and ten technicians of SEVS-PE. In the analysis of this qualitative case study, a KT model was used, overlaid with a theoretical framework of evaluation use and influence. In eight of the studies analyzed, evidence was categorized into five KT phases. Only the stage of evaluating the use of knowledge was not evidenced. Different types of use, dimensions, and levels of influence of the evaluations were reported. Among the changes that occurred, the following were reported: creation of new agendas, development of protocols, changes in work processes, establishment of new workflows, and hiring of new professionals.

### tr-037
This article analyzes road safety on federal highways located in urban areas in the Southern region of Brazil. It seeks to evaluate the occurrence of victims, based on records from the Federal Highway Police (PRF), together with pavement, road geometry, and traffic signage conditions, according to the methodology of the National Confederation of Transport (CNT). Critical urban stretches were highlighted using a geoprocessing program, and, for the delimitation of crashes, concepts based on the Safe System approach were used, which holds that traffic crashes, when they occur, should not cause serious or fatal injuries. When deaths and victims with serious injuries are taken into account, the urban stretches of federal highways in the Southern region were among the most dangerous in Brazil. Most highways in urban areas were classified as "good" with regard to their state of conservation, but several segments concentrated a large number of victims, mainly motorcyclists. As a negative highlight, São José, in Santa Catarina, had the most critical stretch. Of the segments with high crash rates, almost all were close to large urban agglomerations or regional capitals, probably due to the large number of trips generated by socioeconomic activities.

### tr-038
The Cactaceae family includes 1,850 species distributed primarily in the Americas, with Brazil being the third largest center of diversity of the family, with 277 species. In this latter center of diversity, the Boa Nova National Park (PNBN), located in the east of the State of Bahia, Brazil, has an area of about 12,065 ha, including an east-west ecotone, with areas of Atlantic Forest being replaced by vine forest (mata-de-cipó) and, farther west, by Caatinga. As part of the 2011 National Action Plan for the conservation of Cactaceae, this work presents the taxonomic study of Cactaceae occurring in the PNBN, and aims to expand knowledge of the local flora and of the Conservation Units of the State of Bahia. The material was collected during four collecting expeditions over one year, along random trails covering the various environments of the park. Ten genera and 15 species were recorded: Arrojadoa penicillata, Brasilicereus phaeacanthus, Brasiliopuntia brasiliensis, Cereus jamacaru, Cereus sp., Melocactus ernestii, Pereskia aculeata, P. bahiensis, Pilosocereus catingicola, P. pentaedrophorus subsp. robustus, Rhipsalis hileiabaiana, R. russellii, Stephanocereus leucostele, Tacinga funalis, and T. werneri. Within the PNBN, the vine forest (mata-de-cipó) and Caatinga vegetation presented the highest numbers of species, with eight and five species, respectively, while the Atlantic Forest presented a single species.

### tr-039
This article proposes an analysis of the issue of disease and death in artistic representations in painting collections in Belém do Pará between the late nineteenth and early twentieth centuries. To this end, it proposes a cross-cutting reading based on questions posed in the present, during the covid-19 pandemic, as a comparative heuristic resource. It therefore seeks to establish a dialogue between present and past through images, potencies, reverberations, discursive records, and museum exhibitions. Thus, diseases portrayed in painting, such as cancer and tuberculosis, seen in the context of the epidemics of the belle époque, are now analyzed not only within the framework of the comorbidities of the present-day pandemic, but also as a cognitive repertoire of the very history and memory of images in the Amazon.

### tr-040
Considering the particleboard industry, most of it uses synthetic adhesives, especially urea-formaldehyde-based ones, and in this sense, studies that encompass the characteristics of alternative materials for the panel industry become important, especially when considering the final cost of the products. The general objective of this study was to evaluate changes in the chemical structure of the adhesive that could cause possible modifications in its binding properties as a function of replacing the urea-formaldehyde adhesive with lignosulfonate. To this end, the elemental chemical composition of lignosulfonate and its chemical bonds were determined by infrared (IR) spectroscopy and nuclear magnetic resonance (NMR), both pure and in composition with different catalysts; the properties of urea-formaldehyde and its modifications, such as pH, viscosity, and gel time, were also determined. The NMR analysis showed, for lignosulfonate, the same pattern generally obtained for lignin derivatives in terms of chemical bonds. The elemental chemical analysis showed high contents of Mg, Ca, and Na. The addition of lignosulfonate to urea-formaldehyde caused acidification of the adhesive and a reduction in viscosity values. The effect on gel time was also affected; however, it was found that the methodology for this type of polymer should be adapted relative to the traditional method, by subjecting it to a temperature above 90°C. The results led to the conclusion that it is possible to use lignosulfonate as a replacement for urea-formaldehyde adhesive in particleboard.
