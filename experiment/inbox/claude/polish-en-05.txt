### en-041
This study assessed heavy metal concentrations in sweet potato (Ipomoea batatas) and spinach associated with uncontrolled municipal waste, motor vehicle fumes, pesticides, and fertilizer in the urban west region of Zanzibar. Twenty samples were randomly collected from local markets and from three farms located along a roadside in the urban west region. The samples were analysed using energy dispersive X-ray fluorescence at the Tanzania Atomic Energy Commission in Arusha. Essential and toxic elements (Mn, Cu, Fe, As, Zn, Pb, and Ni) were detected in both sweet potato and spinach. Sweet potato had higher concentrations of Cr, Fe, Cd, and Zn, whereas spinach had higher concentrations of Ni, Cr, and Zn. The concentrations of essential elements in the vegetables analysed were within the safety baseline levels for human consumption. However, the concentrations of toxic elements exceeded the FAO/WHO permissible limits. The higher concentrations of toxic elements in sweet potato and spinach might be associated with contamination from municipal waste, chicken manure, motor vehicle fumes, or pesticides. Therefore, vegetable cultivation along roadsides in the urban west region of Zanzibar, together with the use of fertilizer and chicken manure, may introduce high levels of heavy metals into the food chain.

### en-042
This study investigated the genetic diversity of the 'Zuijinxiang' grape and its F1 mutant breeding plants in order to screen excellent mutants with potential breeding value. Fifty mutated single plants obtained from 137Cs-γ-irradiated 'Zuijinxiang' grape seeds were used as research material. SCoT molecular markers were used for genetic diversity and variation analysis, and cluster analysis was performed. The results were as follows. (1) Thirty-six SCoT primers produced abundant polymorphisms with clearly visible bands; the amplification efficiency and polymorphism rate were 100%. (2) A total of 221 bands were amplified by the 36 primers, of which 175 were polymorphic; the average percentage of polymorphism was 80.3%, and the average genetic similarity coefficient was 0.916. (3) The number of observed alleles (Na) ranged from 4 to 8, with an average of 6.1389; the number of effective alleles (Ne) ranged from 1.2772 to 5.6322, with an average of 3.5968; the expected heterozygosity (He) ranged from 0.2192 to 0.8344, with an average of 0.6965; the observed heterozygosity (Ho) ranged from 0.1656 to 0.7808, with an average of 0.3035; Nei's gene diversity index (H) ranged from 0.2170 to 0.8224, with an average of 0.6863; and the Shannon-Wiener index (I) ranged from 0.5186 to 1.8597, with an average of 1.4517. (4) UPGMA clustering of the 51 materials showed that they could be divided into three groups at a genetic distance of 0.856. These results indicate that the irradiation-induced variant germplasm of 'Zuijinxiang' is genetically rich. In addition, SCoT markers can distinguish materials with close genetic distances and can be used for the early identification of grape mutant materials. This study provides a theoretical basis for developing excellent mutant germplasm of 'Zuijinxiang' grape.

### en-043
Silicon and proline play important physiological, metabolic, and functional roles in plants, especially under water deficit. Their application can mitigate the adverse effects of stress in crops by increasing water use efficiency and antioxidant activity. This study aimed to evaluate silicon (Si) as an attenuator of the effects of water deficit on cowpea cultivars, using physiological, biochemical, and growth indicators. The experiment followed a randomized block design with four cultivars (BRS Guariba, BRS Itaim, BRS Aracê, and BRS Rouxinol) and four irrigation treatments, with or without Si application (W100, 100% ETo; W50, 50% ETo; W50+100Si and W50+200Si, with 100 and 200 mg L-1 of silicon, respectively), in a 4 × 4 factorial scheme with five replicates. Leaf water potential, proline concentration, antioxidant enzymes, and growth indicators were evaluated in the cowpea plants. Under water deficit, all cultivars showed reduced leaf water potential, which compromised plant growth. However, Si applications of 200 mg L-1 in BRS Guariba and of 100 and 200 mg L-1 in BRS Itaim minimized the effects of stress by increasing leaf water potential and ascorbate peroxidase activity in both cultivars. Proline concentration increased in the former and decreased in the latter, and growth was maintained in both. Although they did not increase water potential, Si applications of 100 and 200 mg L-1 in BRS Rouxinol and BRS Aracê, respectively, reduced the deleterious effects of stress on growth by regulating enzymatic metabolism and proline.

### en-044
The electrochemical reduction of nitrofurazone (NF) was studied by cyclic voltammetry (CV) and square wave voltammetry (SWV) in a non-aqueous medium, using three different ionic liquids (ILs), BMImTf2N, BMImBF4, and BMMImTf2N, with a carbon fiber microelectrode as the working electrode. In all of them, two reversible cathodic peaks were recorded for NF. At higher frequencies, only one reversible cathodic peak was observed. The reversibility of the systems was also evident by CV, since a reversible redox couple was recorded for this reduction. The reversibility of the systems, with adsorption of product and reagent on the electrode surface, was confirmed, and the number of electrons involved in the reduction indicated the formation of the nitro anion radical followed by its respective dianion. For the first reduction, an EC mechanism (an electrochemical step followed by a chemical step) was proposed for the systems in aprotic medium, in which a probable second-order chemical reaction follows the charge transfer process. The kinetic constants were calculated using the Olmstead and Nicholson model.

### en-045
Subsistence fishing is a confusing and heterogeneous fishery construct. Even so, its connection to human protection compels us to analyze it through the lens of human rights. Using Chile as a case study because of its legal peculiarities, we aim to determine the scope of Chilean legislation on subsistence fishing, integrating international human rights treaties, case law, and reports from United Nations agencies, with regard to three issues. First, we examine how Chilean legislation relates to the right to food and the promotion of decent social conditions. Second, we explain why the prohibition of riggings and propulsion enables the identification of economically precarious users, and how this prohibition relates to vulnerability and poverty as human rights concepts. Finally, we show how the property rights of indigenous peoples and the culture of fisherfolk populations could require their inclusion and preferential treatment in access to subsistence fishing resources. Considering these results, we argue that human rights help to clarify the understanding of subsistence fishing, and we propose partial amendments to the Chilean legislation. Above all, however, human rights introduce protection standards that allow us to see such legislation not as a mere derivation of state privilege, but as an attempt to foster equality: an affirmative action. We conclude by presenting a conceptual approach to Chilean subsistence fishing, suggesting that it could help to unveil new objectives and rights in fishing and even influence the understanding of natural resource allocation.

### en-046
The bearing capacity of saturated soils can be estimated using effective or total stress approaches, which extend the concepts proposed by Terzaghi (1943) and Skempton (1948), respectively. Recent studies have shown that similar approaches (i.e., the Modified Effective Stress Approach, MESA, and the Modified Total Stress Approach, MTSA) can be used to interpret and predict the bearing capacity of unsaturated soils by considering the influence of matric suction. However, a comprehensive discussion of the application of MESA and MTSA in geotechnical engineering practice is lacking in the literature. In this state-of-the-art paper, the background associated with MESA and MTSA is therefore introduced first. The analytical and numerical methods available in the literature for predicting the bearing capacity of unsaturated soils are then revisited. The available methods are explained by categorizing them into two groups, MESA and MTSA, and their applications are illustrated with examples. This state-of-the-art paper aims not only to provide tools for a rational understanding of the bearing capacity of unsaturated soils but also to improve its prediction, so that these approaches can be extended to geotechnical engineering practice.

### en-047
Despite their small share of the economy, agricultural activities are of significant importance in the Porto Alegre metropolitan region (PAMR), Rio Grande do Sul State, Brazil. This has been reflected in efforts to consolidate these activities, especially in the family farm model. To evaluate soil characteristics and to understand the perceptions and assessments of producers, two settlements in the PAMR were visited, and housing and production areas of family farms were selected for this study. Tools such as semi-structured interviews and guided walks were used, and some plots were selected for soil sampling and observation. The results showed that the soils of the settlements have limitations for agricultural use, such as low natural chemical fertility, sandy texture, and drainage restrictions. Local producers can distinguish these characteristics, and comparisons with the land of their places of origin were important for this. Local people more readily understood field approaches, which highlights the need for such tools to foster dialogue among technicians, researchers, and the target population. It was possible to recognize correspondences between local people's observations and the technical-scientific attributes used in land suitability evaluations, showing a rapprochement between these types of knowledge and allowing the participatory planning of sustainable production systems.

### en-048
Mothers' and fathers' conceptualizations of joy, sadness, anger, fear, pride, and shame were assessed, as were their beliefs regarding the importance of children's expression of these emotions and their connection with the profiles of autonomy, relatedness, and autonomous-relatedness. Sixty mother-father dyads with children up to three years old participated in the study. Questionnaires on parents' conceptualizations of emotions were used. Most participants considered joy an important emotion for children of their kids' age to express (with an individual motivational character). However, anger, pride, and shame were associated with older children. Mothers' and fathers' conceptualizations and beliefs did not diverge. The autonomous-related self model correlated positively with the importance that mothers and fathers attributed to all the emotions studied.

### en-049
For manufactured homes, the Light Gauge Steel Frame system lacks an in-depth study of the relationship between its assembly techniques and the behavior of assembly-line-based production systems. This article therefore presents the development of a flow shop production system using assembly lines for the production of Light Gauge Steel Frame modular houses. The main result was that the system was able to deliver a house in slightly more than eight working hours, using four-hour cycles. An experimental research procedure with computational simulation was used. The abstracted model of the designed system was simulated, and its behavior was analyzed with respect to improvement hypotheses proposed in four different scenarios. The results are relevant because they fill gaps in the body of knowledge by using Light Gauge Steel Frame as a technology for producing manufactured houses on assembly lines, and they also provide performance and design parameters for implementing the proposed solution in the real estate market.

### en-050
Thermotherapy of sugarcane setts is currently the primary management method for Leifsonia xyli subsp. xyli (Lxx) in Brazil. When the setts are immersed, the enzymes and proteins of the bacterial cell are denatured without harming the sett buds. Because the bacterium may escape detection and survive thermotherapy, possibly resulting in asymptomatic seedlings, this study aimed to detect Leifsonia xyli subsp. xyli in sugarcane setts using molecular techniques and different time and temperature combinations, with or without the addition of antibiotics. Conventional PCR detected Lxx DNA only in the positive control, which consisted of a highly susceptible plant with a high bacterial concentration. Using nested PCR, Lxx DNA was detected in all treatments. Thus, none of the thermotherapy treatments eliminated Lxx from the setts, and the use of kasugamycin also did not eliminate the bacterium, although it reduced the bacterial population in the tested treatments. These results confirm that nested PCR is a useful tool for detecting this phytobacterium in setts to be used as seedlings.
