### en-011
In this article, residual indicators were used to characterize the quality of the numerical solution of the advection-diffusion-reaction equation in a saturated porous medium. Both large and small advection regimes were considered. Small advection was exemplified by a problem with constant data, whereas non-constant data were considered for the large advection regime. In the latter case, residual quantities associated with the data must be incorporated into the residual estimates related to the spatial approximation, and an auxiliary problem must be solved to correctly obtain the temporal estimates. Presenting the residual indicators as a surface over the finite element mesh provides a detailed view of the regions that require refinement, allows the effect of each estimate on the composition of the global estimator to be inferred, and makes it possible to follow the evolution of the residual surfaces as the contaminant front advances during the simulation. In turn, the numerical values of the indicators make it possible to delimit the elements to be refined, to compare the magnitudes of the contributions with one another and across different meshes, and to better understand the composition of the global estimates.

### en-012
We present a checklist of Odonata (Insecta) species occurring in a fragment of Seasonal Deciduous Forest (Atlantic Forest biome) in the central region of the state of Rio Grande do Sul (RS), southern Brazil, together with a list of the odonate species recorded in this phytoecological region of the state. In addition, we comment on the seasonal distribution of the species occurring in the study area. Two streams and seven farm ponds located in the middle course of the Jacuí River basin were surveyed between December 2007 and February 2009. Overall, we recorded 49 species from 21 genera and six families. Argia serva Hagen in Selys, 1865 (Coenagrionidae) was recorded for the first time in the state, raising to 183 the total number of Odonata species known to occur in Rio Grande do Sul. The number of species recorded in the study area corresponds to ~26% of the known Odonata diversity in RS. Libellulidae was the most species-rich family (22 species, ~45% of the total), followed by Coenagrionidae (18 species, 37% of the total). The checklist for the Seasonal Deciduous Forest in RS indicated the occurrence of 83 Odonata species in this phytoecological region (~45% of the known odonate species in the state). This high diversity may be related to the density of the vegetation structure. In the study area, 20 species were found in streams and 45 in farm ponds. Species occurrence showed marked seasonal patterns, with 88% of the species recorded from summer to autumn and no species detected in streams in winter. Moreover, 70% of the species were recorded in only one or two seasons in farm ponds, while 65% occurred in only one season in streams. These results indicate that the life cycle of Odonata in southern Brazil is strongly influenced by seasonal patterns in temperature.

### en-013
Introduction: Peripheral vestibular disorders can lead to cognitive deficits and are more common in elderly patients. Objective: To evaluate and correlate cognitive, balance, and gait aspects in elderly women with chronic peripheral vestibular dizziness, and to compare them with elderly women without vestibular disorders. Methods: Twenty-two women with episodes of peripheral vestibular dizziness for at least six months participated in the study. They were categorized by dizziness severity as moderate (n = 11) or severe (n = 11). The control group (n = 11) comprised women with no vestibulopathy, light-headedness, or dizziness. Cognitive assessments and semi-static and dynamic balance assessments were performed with the Balance Master (Neurocom International, Inc., Clackamas, OR), while the Dizziness Handicap Inventory provided a score for symptom severity. Inferential statistics and correlation analyses were applied to the cognitive, balance, and stability variables across the groups. Results: The group with severe dizziness showed higher sway velocity of the center of pressure in the anteroposterior direction, shorter step length, and slower gait than the control group. Regarding the cognitive variables, the group with severe dizziness symptoms showed significant correlations with stability and gait variables. Conclusion: The relationship between cognitive aspects, balance, and gait was stronger in women with severe dizziness than in those without vestibulopathy.

### en-014
This study aims to identify the different practices found in innovation environments belonging to National Innovation Systems with distinct maturity levels: France (advanced) and Brazil (in development). A case study compared the Technological Park of São José dos Campos and the Pôle de Compétitivité HYDREOS across seven dimensions: governance, strategy, external relationships, external environment, legal context, infrastructure, and funding mechanisms. The results support the initial premise that the environment embedded in the mature system presents a more comprehensive set of the studied dimensions. The main differences identified, which also represent improvement opportunities for the Brazilian environment, concern external relationships, strategy, funding mechanisms, and governance. Most of these differences directly affect the degree of interaction among the members of the innovation environment and between them and external actors, the diversity of actors, and the relationship rules, which are key elements of a national innovation system.

### en-015
Research in education indicates that the Philosophy for Children (P4C) curriculum is instrumental in achieving important educational objectives. Yet it is precisely this instrumentalist conception of P4C that has been challenged by a second generation of P4C scholars. Among other things, these scholars argue that P4C must remain vigilant toward, and avoid subscribing to, (1) developmentalism and (2) a reductive identification of thinking with rationality. On the contrary, they suggest that P4C must give voice to childhood, allowing it to enter into genuine dialogue with adulthood. Scholars who defend a non-reductive and non-instrumentalist approach to P4C highlight the significance of play in philosophy sessions with children. In this paper, I examine the extent to which the philosophical inquiry that takes place in the context of P4C can be understood as a playful activity. I submit that Fink's account of play can help us better understand what we mean by play, which in turn can help us examine the compatibility between the activities of P4C and play. In the first part of the paper, I examine some of the basic ideas of P4C and raise the question of the compatibility of philosophical inquiry and play. In the second part, I engage in a philosophical appreciation of play by drawing on the work of Eugen Fink. In the final part, I show how play, understood along Fink's lines, is compatible with philosophical inquiry as practiced in school settings.

### en-016
Experimental measurements of systems with slow gas transport kinetics are generally considered easier than measurements of very fast systems. However, when the transport process has time constants of the order of several hours, not only the measurements but also the analysis and interpretation of the data become challenging, making the determination of the correct time constant non-trivial. In this work, we used measurements of CO2 diffusion in Na,Cs-Rho crystals, carried out using the zero length column (ZLC) technique, as a case study for the application of the technique to very slow adsorption processes. The system, which has a time constant of the order of 8 h, demonstrates the importance of the partial loading approach for unambiguously determining the time constant from the analysis of ZLC desorption curves. The traditional analysis is refined by using the nonlinear ZLC model to account for the isotherm nonlinearity that results in a concentration-dependent diffusivity. Finally, the method proposed by Cavalcante is used to confirm the three-dimensional diffusion path of the system.

### en-017
General aspects of the chemistry of dithiocarbamates, their metal complexes, and their interactions with important enzymes: a brief review. Dithiocarbamates (DTCs) comprise a series of compounds belonging to the class of 1,1-dithiolates. The use of these organic molecules in the coordination chemistry of several metals has been well explored in recent decades, along with their potential applications in medicinal chemistry. Given our expertise in this field, including the synthesis of new DTCs, their coordination chemistry with various metal cations, and their biocidal activities, we summarize in this manuscript the outcomes of selected investigations reported in the literature on the interactions of DTCs or their complexes with key enzymes such as carbonic anhydrases, superoxide dismutase, urease, tyrosinase, glutathione-related enzymes, caspases, and dehydrogenases. We first provide a brief introduction to the chemistry of DTCs, covering their electrochemistry, kinetics of formation, solubility, and stability toward solvation. The following sections summarize the results of experiments on the inhibition of these enzymes by DTCs and some of their coordination complexes. Final remarks and conclusions are presented at the end of the manuscript.

### en-018
Language attrition has become a vibrant research topic because of its implications for theoretical linguistics, including the organization of linguistic information in the brain and language processing. Insights from this area also affect the study of second language (L2) development and can inform language teaching. Because language attrition may be valuable for the study of language retention and maintenance, it can contribute to language teaching with long-lasting results, more effective planning, and better syllabus design. In this context, as studies exploring L2 attrition are still limited, this article provides a synthesis of research in the area. Drawing on the Dynamic Model of Multilingualism and the Threshold Hypothesis, it also explores the implications of L2 attrition research for language teaching, with a view to establishing more informed language practices and policies.

### en-019
Data from field experiments conducted in Festival table grape vineyards in Petrolina, PE, Brazil, from October 2009 to November 2010, were used to evaluate the influence of plastic cover on the productivity and economic profitability of viticulture in the São Francisco River Valley. Three treatments were studied: uncovered canopy (UC), plastic cover placed 50 cm above the canopy (PC50), and plastic cover placed 100 cm above the canopy (PC100). The results indicated that the greater availability of radiative fluxes at berry height in PC100 contributed to higher productivity, whereas PC50 had the lowest availability of radiative fluxes at berry height and a much lower productivity. The yield obtained in PC100 exceeded those of UC and PC50 by 11 t ha-1 and 12.3 t ha-1, respectively. PC100 also had the lowest amount of berry defects, which contributed to higher total revenue. By contrast, PC50 had markedly lower productivity, which represented considerable economic losses.

### en-020
Ethnic Tibetan farm and herder households (FHH) in Gansu Province, China, are stricken by poverty. Solving the poverty problem in this and other poverty pockets across China is of particular importance for the country's political stability, ethnic unity, social well-being, and ecological security. This paper assesses the multidimensional poverty of ethnic Tibetan FHH in Gansu Province, China, using the Alkire-Foster method. Twelve indicators are included in the multidimensional poverty indicator system. The results showed that (1) in the single-indicator measure, adult family members having at least a 9th-grade education (x3), home toilet type (x7), and labor availability (x2) showed a higher incidence of deprivation; (2) many FHH in the Tibetan areas of Gansu Province face multidimensional poverty, but the proportion of extreme poverty is very small; and (3) in terms of contribution rate, adult family members having at least a 9th-grade education (x3), labor availability (x2), self-health assessment (x1), home toilet type (x7), and school-age children dropout rate (x4) occupied the top five positions under the equal weight to dimensions (DEW) method.
