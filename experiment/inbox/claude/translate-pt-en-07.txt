### tr-061
ASTM A572 Grade 50 steel is used in the manufacture of welded assemblies for wind turbines; for this reason, stress relief is of great importance for correcting the impacts caused by welding processes, as well as for understanding the final properties obtained through this process. The objective of this study is to compare the mechanical properties and residual stresses (hole-drilling method) in ASTM A572 Grade 50 steel samples welded by the submerged arc process, subjected to stress relief in the thermal condition (PWHT) and in the as-welded condition (SST), following the normative requirements of AWS D1.1-2015. The results for the PWHT condition show lower hardness, yield strength, tensile strength, and residual stresses, and higher elongation and impact absorbed energy values than the SST condition. Both conditions passed the side bend test. The results obtained demonstrate the importance of the stress relief process, which minimizes the undesirable effects of welding processes on components.

### tr-062
The objective of this study was to verify the influence of supplementation with different combinations of organic minerals on performance and carcass characteristics, as well as to evaluate its economic viability, for finishing pigs. Eighty-eight castrated males were used, divided among four treatments: a control with a basal diet and supplementation with CrFe, MgSe, and CrFeMgSe, during the 28 days prior to slaughter. The experimental design was a randomized block design, with the experimental unit represented by two animals. Animals supplemented with CrFeMgSe showed worse final weight, average daily gain, and feed intake throughout the period, with a consequent reduction in carcass weight, lower loin depth, smaller loin eye area, and a lower amount of lean meat in the carcass. There was an increase in diet cost for the CrFe, MgSe, and CrFeMgSe groups, respectively. The use of chromium plus iron and magnesium plus selenium, combined in pairs, does not alter performance parameters or carcass characteristics. The combination of the four minerals, however, worsens these characteristics and increases diet cost.

### tr-063
Meteorological conditions unfavorable to pollutant dispersion, together with high atmospheric pollutant emission rates and complex topography, frequently favor the occurrence of high pollutant concentrations in the Metropolitan Region of Rio de Janeiro (MRRJ). In this context, this study examined the influence of meteorological conditions in two periods in which high concentrations of O3 and PM10 occurred on consecutive days in the MRRJ. The study used the Weather Research and Forecasting Model (WRF), a mesoscale numerical model, to simulate the meteorological conditions and variables favorable to the persistence of these episodes, examining the influence of the South Atlantic Subtropical Anticyclone (SASA), high air temperatures, low wind speeds, and absence of cloud cover, in agreement with the observed data. Visual analysis showed that the model was effective in representing the SASA, precipitation, and cloud cover. The model showed a positive Pearson linear correlation between simulated and observed temperature above 0.71 in both periods at all 8 meteorological stations, and a positive Pearson linear correlation between simulated and observed relative humidity above 0.72. The mean error (ME) calculation indicated that, in general, the model underestimated air temperature and overestimated relative humidity. The application of statistical methods to the observed and simulated data showed that the model has skill in representing the meteorological variables for most of the stations.

### tr-064
Introduction: The identification of complications of primary palatoplasty may vary among professionals from different health areas due to the lack of standardization in fistula classification. This study aimed to verify the consensus between plastic surgery (PS) and speech-language pathology (SLP) professionals regarding the occurrence of fistula, as reported at the same craniofacial service. Methods: An analysis was performed of the records of the PS and SLP areas regarding the presence and location of fistulas, as reported in 466 medical records of patients with a history of unilateral complete cleft lip and palate. To compare the findings between both areas, a gold standard verification of fistula occurrence (FO) was established by an experienced plastic surgeon. Results: The PS area reported that 25% of the 466 patients had a fistula, compared with 37% reported by SLP, while FO indicated a fistula in 35% of cases. Kappa statistics revealed fair agreement between FO and PS (r=0.32) and substantial agreement between FO and SLP (r=0.63). Conclusion: Disagreement was observed between the speech-language pathology and plastic surgery areas regarding the occurrence and location of fistula after primary palatoplasty at the same craniofacial center. The data point to the need to create and implement a standardized fistula classification system that can be used effectively by craniofacial teams, favoring scientific evidence on cleft lip and palate treatment outcomes.

### tr-065
Statistics show that, worldwide, women are the main providers of human life reproduction work, which involves household and care tasks, defined as unpaid work. Its value and importance to society must be recognized. One way of doing this is to show the volume of wealth, in the form of goods and services, generated by this invisible work, through its measurement/valuation. To this end, it is necessary to create a Satellite Account for unpaid work, fulfilling one of the objectives proposed by the UN in its list of Millennium Goals. Accordingly, this study proposes a discussion of a social indicator of unpaid work for Brazil to be incorporated into the National Accounts. This paper discusses the meaning of Satellite Accounts for gender statistics, highlighting the economic importance of household chores and care work, not only for families but for society as a whole, and emphasizing the difficulties in reconciling work and family for those responsible for unpaid work. Finally, the text develops a methodological proposal to estimate unpaid work in Brazil that is compatible with the national accounts methodology.

### tr-066
Cassava black rot caused by Scytalidium lignicola has become an important disease in producing states due to large production losses. Therefore, the objective of the present study was to evaluate the effect of sources and doses of organic matter, alone and added to sandy soil, on the suppressiveness of mycelial growth of S. lignicola CMM 1098. Two experiments were carried out: the first aimed to evaluate the effect of extracts of organic materials (poultry litter, goat manure, cattle manure, and earthworm humus) incorporated into potato dextrose agar (PDA) culture medium at concentrations of 10, 20, 30, and 40% (v/v), and the second evaluated the effect of the organic materials mixed with sandy soil, using the same sources and doses. Poultry litter was the most efficient source in inhibiting the mycelial growth of S. lignicola at a 40% dose, with a mycelial growth inhibition of 69.75% only when the extracts were not autoclaved. Non-autoclaved poultry litter at doses equal to or greater than 30% was effective in suppressing the mycelial growth of S. lignicola CMM 1098. Non-autoclaved sandy soil with added sources and doses of organic matter is highly efficient in suppressing the growth of S. lignicola CMM 1098.

### tr-067
In this study, the microstructure and intergranular corrosion resistance of ferritic stainless steels with additions of Nb, Ti, and (Ti+Nb) subjected to TIG welding were investigated. The structural characterization of the weld beads was carried out by optical microscopy (OM), scanning electron microscopy (SEM), and energy dispersive spectroscopy (EDS). Susceptibility to sensitization was evaluated qualitatively through Practice W of the ASTM A763 standard and quantitatively through the DL-EPR technique. A martensite network was observed at the grain boundaries in the weld region of the unstabilized steel, and a completely ferritic microstructure was observed in the samples of steels with stabilizer additions. Practice W revealed generalized corrosion inside the grains of AISI 430 steel and the presence of ditches in AISI 430 Ti; concomitantly, the DL-EPR technique showed that these steels were sensitized by welding. In contrast, the samples of steels with additions of Nb and (Ti+Nb) maintained good corrosion resistance, remaining free of sensitization after welding.

### tr-068
The present study aims to analyze Primary Health Care (PHC) as a learning environment for medical students. A descriptive cross-sectional study with a qualitative approach was carried out through four focus groups with interns from the four medical schools in Fortaleza (Ceará, Brazil). The method of interpretation of meanings was used, and two empirical categories were identified. For the students, Primary Care is an important teaching setting for correlating theory and practice, but without recognizing its importance within the Unified Health System (SUS). Thus, the defense of the SUS is not included as an important principle of their profession. However, incentives for teaching in Primary Care and the development of humanistic aspects during training would be motivating factors for working in this area.

### tr-069
In critical situations resulting from low hydroelectric reservoir levels, the control of the Brazilian electric sector is based on hydrothermal planning that depends on future inflows to hydroelectric power plants (HPPs). Hydrological uncertainties are considered through synthetic scenarios of sequences of natural inflow energies to the subregions. Two computational models are used to optimize the system: NEWAVE (medium and long term) and DECOMP (short term). NEWAVE is an energy planning model applied to the National Interconnected System (SIN) that uses Stochastic Dual Dynamic Programming (SDDP) to determine the operating policy that minimizes the marginal cost of operation over a horizon of up to 5 years. The objective of this work is to evaluate the performance of NEWAVE, through the NWLISTOP functionality, in projecting Natural Inflow Energy (NIE) and Stored Energy (SE) in the Northeast (NE) Subsystem for the years 2012 to 2016. It was found that the model tends to overestimate the NIE and SE values. The results indicate the need for techniques that allow the optimization of long-term operation planning, especially in episodes of extreme drought.

### tr-070
The semiarid region of the State of Rio Grande do Norte can be divided into two main geoenvironmental units: the Sertaneja Depression and the Potiguar Sedimentary Basin. Although the flora of sandy sedimentary basins is usually different from that of crystalline terrains, part of the Potiguar Basin has stony paleo-alluvial deposits with rounded pebbles, imposing different edaphic conditions on the flora. The Açu National Forest is located in a stretch of the edge of the Potiguar Basin with pebble deposits, which make the soil stony. The objective of this study was to characterize the phytosociological structure of the caatinga in the FLONA and its surroundings and to understand the biogeographic links of the local flora in relation to other Caatinga areas. We selected areas within and around the FLONA and sampled 1,500 woody individuals belonging to 33 species. The biogeographic links show that the stony edge of the Potiguar Basin is closer to the flora of crystalline terrains than to the sandy terrains of other sandy sedimentary basins.
