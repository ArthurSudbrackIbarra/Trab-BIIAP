### tr-051
The largest volume of grape juice produced in Brazil comes from the Isabel cultivar. However, blending with other cultivars is necessary to improve juice quality, which increases the demand for other cultivars, especially Bordô. Therefore, the objective of this study was to evaluate the influence of the cultivars BRS Rúbea, BRS Cora, BRS Violeta, BRS Carmem, BRS Magna, and Bordô, in blends with Isabel, on the physicochemical, colorimetric, and sensory characteristics of juices produced in the Vale do Rio do Peixe Region, Santa Catarina State, Brazil. The juices were prepared at Epagri – Videira Experimental Station, Santa Catarina State, as follows: (ISA) 100% Isabel; (ISA+RUB) 70% Isabel + 30% Rúbea; (ISA+COR) 70% Isabel + 30% Cora; (ISA+VIO) 70% Isabel + 30% Violeta; (ISA+CAR) 70% Isabel + 30% Carmem; (ISA+MAG) 70% Isabel + 30% Magna; (ISA+BOR) 70% Isabel + 30% Bordô, and were evaluated for physicochemical composition, colorimetric composition, and sensory profile. The highest titratable acidity was found in ISA+MAG, followed by ISA+BOR. The ISA+VIO juices had the highest mean soluble solids content, differing only from ISA+CAR, which also had the lowest acidity among the treatments. The ISA+VIO and ISA+CAR juices had higher soluble solids content than the 100% Isabel juice. The lowest lightness was observed in the ISA+VIO juices, followed by ISA+CAR. Regarding sensory attributes, significant differences were observed only for color and acidity. The ISA+VIO, ISA+CAR, and ISA+BOR juices received the highest scores for color. All the cultivars tested influenced the colorimetric characteristics of the juice, proving to be alternatives for improving the color of Isabel juices. Sensorially, only color and acidity were influenced by the different blends.

### tr-052
The use of rainwater in buildings is an attractive alternative as a water source. However, to enable its use, rainwater must be treated to reach the required quality. Filters are widely used and come in several models. In order to evaluate different filtration systems, the parameters obtained with each treatment unit surveyed were compared with the values recommended by ABNT (2007). The efficiencies of the systems were also compared with one another. To this end, a literature review was carried out based on a search of the existing literature. The aim was to organize information on the state of the art of rainwater treatment units worldwide and to contribute to the consolidation of the use of these systems. A lack of standardization in methods and parameters was identified, which hinders an accurate analysis of which models would be most suitable for possible usage scenarios. It was also found that the water quality standard required by the Brazilian standard was not met in any case. Nevertheless, the models studied showed promising results, achieving high removal percentages, mostly ranging from 60% to 100%.

This article discusses the adherence of industrial taxonomies from the evolutionary tradition to the reality of Brazilian manufacturing industry, based on the selection of indicators of innovative activities for the year 2014. ### tr-054
The large volume of coal ash accumulated in the storage basins of the Energia Pecém Thermoelectric Power Plant has created a demand for research proposing different ways of using this waste, so as to give this material a final destination. The use of ash in dry covers for waste deposits proved to be a viable alternative, owing to the existence of studies on the subject and the possibility of absorbing a significant amount of the volume of waste produced. The study presented corresponds to the second stage of a research project that verified the feasibility of applying coal ash in cover systems for municipal solid waste landfills. Based on the results of a preliminary numerical modeling, three soil columns were built, representing three cover models: Column A – an upper layer of natural soil (30 cm) and a layer of sealing material consisting of the MS70C30 soil-ash mixture (30 cm, with 70% soil and 30% ash); Column B – a layer of natural soil (30 cm) and a layer of sealing material made of composite ash (30 cm); and Column C – a single layer of natural soil (30 cm), representing the cover found at the Sanitary Landfill of the Municipality of Caucaia. Column A was instrumented with three moisture sensors. Two infiltration tests were carried out in each column, evaluating the percolated volumes in each system and the saturation profiles of the instrumented column (Column A). When evaluating the percolated volume in each test, the MS70C30 mixture (Column A) was found to show the best performance, resulting in a percolated volume far lower than that of the other two models. The material also maintained high saturation conditions, above 90% throughout almost all of the tests, a condition necessary to reduce biogas leakage from inside the system.

### tr-055
The objective of this article is to examine the factors associated with seeking preventive health services among the Brazilian adult population. The sample consisted of participants in the National Health Survey (2013) who reported seeking a health service in the previous 15 days, classified as "treatment/diagnosis" or "preventive." The explanatory variables included sex, age group, skin color, marital status, schooling, household location, private health insurance coverage, and length of enrollment in the Family Health Strategy (FHS). Associations were assessed using prevalence ratios (PR), obtained from the Poisson model, taking into account the complexity of the sampling design. The sample consisted of 32,377 individuals who sought health services, 12.94% of them for "preventive" actions. Seeking this type of care was more frequent among women and less frequent among older individuals, those who did not live with a partner, and those with lower schooling. Not having private health insurance was associated with lower use of prevention. Being enrolled in the FHS was not associated with seeking preventive actions. Despite the efforts and the recognition of the importance of prevention, most individuals still seek health services for treatment.

The central theme of this text is the discourses on development surrounding the Tripartite Cooperation Program for the Agricultural Development of the Tropical Savannah in Mozambique – ProSavana, a joint cooperation project of the Brazilian, Japanese, and Mozambican governments. The objective is to interpret how the idea of "emptiness" is produced and reinforced in the program's official promotional material, as well as in its Master Plan. As a secondary source, some interviews with key interlocutors were also used. The reflection draws on the theoretical-methodological framework of French-school discourse analysis in dialogue with critical perspectives on development. The analysis points to the (re)production of the idea that the Mozambican savannah is an "empty place," uninhabited and awaiting development, regardless of the people who are there and their own ways of existing in the world. Moreover, although ProSavana is not formally intended as a "sustainable development" program, the imperative to mobilize it as such is evident. However, this appears to be a futile attempt, given the repetition of practices despite the innovation in the predominant discourse, which implies nothing in terms of sustainability.

### tr-059
The incidence of degradation agents on buildings gives rise to degradation mechanisms that lead to the development of anomalies, which reduce the performance of building systems. In this context, investigating the relationship between anomalies, agents, and mechanisms is fundamental to providing support for service life studies and for the development of more durable materials and elements. The objective of this study is to investigate the severity and extent of degradation of mortar-clad facades, identifying patterns that may condition the degradation of this element. This approach is based on the inspection of buildings in use, and for this purpose the Degradation Measurement Method is employed. The methodology is applied to the study of 90 facade samples from buildings aged between 20 and 60 years located in Brasília, Brazil. The analyses are based on degradation indicators, quantified from the variables investigated, specifically solar orientation and facade zones. The results allow the identification of typical degradation patterns. Cracking is the anomaly that most degrades the facades. The top is the facade zone where the greatest severity of degradation occurs. The greatest incidence of degradation, in turn, occurs in the continuous wall zone. The north and south orientations are the most degraded.

### tr-060
In recent years, the agricultural frontier of oil palm in the Amazon region has continued to advance, causing changes in land cover in this region. Given the need to understand the influence of this crop on surface albedo, this study aims to compare the fluctuations in surface albedo in an area of oil palm cultivation with those of pasture, forest, and secondary vegetation. To make this comparison, orbital data were used, based on the MCD43A3 product of the Terra/MODIS satellite for the years 2015 and 2016. The samples selected for the study area showed that oil palm had little variation, with an estimated mean value of α = 0.14, being statistically distinct from the other land uses and covers, with albedo ranked as: Pasture > Oil palm > Secondary vegetation > Forest. This demonstrates that possible conversions from one cover to another may influence the surface radiation balance; that is, if oil palm cultivation continues to advance over pasture areas, this would decrease surface albedo and consequently lead to changes in the microclimate, whereas if the expansion occurs over areas of secondary vegetation or forest, a reversal will occur, which may contribute to climate change.
