### tr-011
This article seeks to problematize the advance of the ultraneoliberal model present in the Bolsonaro government and its impacts on Brazilian social security policy, as a public policy and, as such, a duty of the State. The advance of reactionary counter-reforms destroys the essential pillars of social security policy: health, social insurance (pensions), and social assistance. The chosen methodology consists of bibliographic research based on scientific works published in articles and books, as well as newspapers and magazines on the subject. The ultraneoliberal agenda imposes an unprecedented persecution of historically conquered rights. In times of the Covid-19 pandemic, the contradictions of the policy of denying rights become evident. Bolsonarism has implemented necropolitics as official policy, which stems from an authoritarian power to define who must die and who deserves to live, further deepening the social barbarism against the working class.

### tr-012
Seed coat and water content may affect seed tolerance to heat shocks, as may the seeds' environment of origin. To test these possibilities, seeds of Plathymenia reticulata and Stryphnodendron adstringens were collected in the north of the State of Minas Gerais (representing dry savanna) and in the northeast of the State of Mato Grosso (representing wet savanna) in the Cerrado biome, and their responses to heat shocks of 110, 140, and 170 ºC for 2.5 minutes were evaluated under different conditions: intact, scarified, and imbibed after scarification. Scarified and imbibed seeds germinated more rapidly for both species. Dry savanna seeds showed lower initial water content and slower germination for both species, but greater tolerance to heat shocks than wet savanna seeds. P. reticulata seeds did not survive the 140 ºC treatment, but dry savanna S. adstringens seeds withstood the 170 ºC treatment, regardless of whether they were scarified and/or imbibed. It is concluded that P. reticulata seeds are less tolerant to heat shocks than S. adstringens seeds, that dry seeds are more tolerant to heat shocks than imbibed seeds, and that seeds from dry savannas are more tolerant to heat shocks than seeds from wet environments.

### tr-013
This article presents a discussion of the relationship between territoriality and intersectionality based on the experience of Black Brazilian women, throughout the historical process that gave rise to a long trajectory of struggle against racism and sexism. Bibliographic and documentary research was used to discuss the territories of the body, the home, and the city, understood as spaces of oppression and resistance. ### tr-014
Currently, several mortars are available for application in the construction industry. These mortars may be based on different binders, have different physical and mechanical properties, and even other specific properties. However, the correct selection of a mortar is becoming increasingly difficult. Thus, there is a need to classify different mortars based on specific criteria and a single procedure. In this way, it will be possible to identify the most appropriate mortars according to their function. In this work, a material classification procedure developed by Czarnecki and Lukowski, based on a utility function, was used. The classification procedure transforms the experimental results of several properties into a single numerical value. In this way, construction products can be classified according to their individual properties or an optimized combination of several properties. Thus, the main objective of this study is the classification of mortars based on different binders with incorporation of phase change materials (PCM). The binders studied were hydraulic lime, gypsum, and cement. For each binder, three distinct mortars were developed: a reference mortar (0% PCM), a mortar with 40% PCM incorporation, and a mortar with 40% PCM and 1% fibers incorporation. Based on the results, it was possible to conclude that the incorporation of PCM affects the overall performance of the mortars.

### tr-015
Background Doxorubicin is associated with cardiotoxicity and late cardiac morbidity. Heme is related to cellular oxidative stress. However, its specific regulation in cardiomyocytes under the effects of doxorubicin has not yet been documented. Objective Our objective is to evaluate the changes in rate-limiting enzymes in the heme metabolic pathway under the effect of doxorubicin. Methods H9c2 cardiomyocytes were treated with doxorubicin at different concentrations (1, 2, 5, and 10 μM, respectively). Real-time PCR and Western Blot assays were used to determine protein and mRNA expression for four crucial enzymes (ALAS1, ALAS2, HOX-1, and HOX-2) that regulate cellular heme metabolism, and heme levels were detected by ELISA. A p<0.01 was considered significant. Results We observed a dose-dependent pattern of change in heme levels in H9c2 cells, with the highest level at a doxorubicin concentration of 5 μM, which occurred synchronously with the highest level of upregulation of ALAS1, as well as of the degradative enzymes HOX-1 and HOX-2, in protein and mRNA expression. In contrast, we observed that ALAS2 was gradually downregulated, inversely proportional to doxorubicin concentrations. Conclusion Increased ALAS1 expression may play a role in the elevation of heme levels when H9c2 cardiomyocytes are exposed to doxorubicin, and may be a therapeutic target for doxorubicin-induced myocardial toxicity. (Arq Bras Cardiol. 2021; 116(2):315-322)

### tr-016
This study aimed to map intense rainfall with durations of 10, 30, 720, and 1440 minutes, associated with return periods of 10, 50, and 100 years, for the State of Tocantins. Historical series from 95 rain gauge stations located in the State of Tocantins and its vicinity were used, with a standardized period from 1983 to 2013. A geostatistical study was conducted, evaluating spherical, exponential, and Gaussian semivariogram models, fitted by the weighted least squares method. From this study, the model that presented the lowest mean error based on cross-validation was applied to the ordinary kriging mapping process. The geostatistical techniques performed well, with the spherical model standing out for mapping annual maximum daily precipitation, and the Gaussian model for the rainfall calculated by the disaggregation methodology. It was also found that the areas where the most severe intense rainfall occurs in the state are Bico do Papagaio (north), Ilha do Bananal (southwest), and the northwest of the state.

### tr-017
Rupture of an abdominal aortic aneurysm is an event with high mortality, and its treatment in these cases is a medical emergency. Endovascular treatment of these aneurysms has become established as a minimally invasive alternative to classic open surgery, becoming the first-choice option. However, 20 to 50% of patients with abdominal aortic aneurysm do not have favorable anatomy for endovascular treatment due to the presence of a short neck or involvement of visceral branches by the aneurysm. We report the case of a 70-year-old female patient who underwent endovascular repair of a ruptured juxtarenal aneurysm with implantation of parallel stents for the renal arteries (chimney technique). Clinical data and procedural details are presented. Technical success was achieved, and no postoperative complications were reported.

### tr-018
The objective of this study was to test the feasibility of using the vaginal route in bovine females for the administration of oxytocin from specific formulations. Oxytocin was used in combination with absorption enhancers and in injectable form (for comparison). Seven formulations were produced, all with an oxytocin concentration of 5 IU/mL. The experimental design was an 8x8 Latin square (eight days, eight treatments, and eight cycling heifers). Oxytocin was administered by the vaginal route, and injectable oxytocin by the intravenous route, each containing 10 IU. Blood samples (n=7) were collected from each animal at time 0, before treatment administration, and every three minutes up to 18 minutes. Oxytocin was extracted from plasma using a C-18 chromatographic column and measured using the commercial Oxytocin EIA kit. The results obtained were evaluated by nonparametric statistical analysis using the Kruskal-Wallis test to compare means. Oxytocin concentration differed between time 0 and 12 minutes (p<0.05) for the oxytocin in glycerin formulation. It was concluded that absorption occurred through the vaginal route for this formulation. Further studies will be necessary for its effective use in the management of bovine females.

### tr-019
The places occupied by discussions in the curriculum say a great deal about the intentionality and the interests surrounding the guiding documents of Brazilian education. In view of this, and based on the theoretical framework related to complex thinking and critical Environmental Education, this study addresses the identification of the place occupied by environmental education in the National Common Curricular Base (BNCC) and in the National Common Base for the initial and continuing education of Basic Education teachers (BNC-Formação). Through documentary study and in light of Discursive Textual Analysis, it is analyzed that what is in fact outlined in the aforementioned documents is a non-place for environmental education, thus converging toward the silencing of the precepts of critical Environmental Education in Basic Education and Higher Education, the latter with regard to teacher education programs (licenciatura). In this sense, the results revealed that socio-environmental discussion is subject to generalization in the BNCC and to invisibilization in the BNC-Formação, thereby demonstrating how such documents are configured as instruments of an education that reproduces the interests of the dominant order and the status quo and is rooted in the simplistic and fragmenting paradigm of knowledge production, which makes a critical and complex perception of reality impossible.

The intention of this article is to rethink such discursivity, considering that the conjuncture of 2020 updates some of those debates linked to geoeconomic dependence. Drawing on the recent archive of the Casa de Rui Barbosa and on poems by Marília Garcia, Ana Estaregui, and Priscilla Menezes, I propose to examine a relationship between ways of reading the archive and the land, or between pedagogies of reading and extractive practices. Following the paths of Santiago's debates, read alongside Josefina Ludmer, we can situate the emergence of an expanded concept of extraction that makes it possible to think about some variations in Latin American discourse.
