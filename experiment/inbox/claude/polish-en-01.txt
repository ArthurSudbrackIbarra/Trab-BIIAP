### en-001
Objective: To analyze the association between depression and various health behaviors and to examine whether these associations differ by sex or income. Methods: This cross-sectional study used data from 65,803 Brazilian adults (aged 18–59 years) interviewed in the 2019 National Health Survey. Depression was assessed using the Patient Health Questionnaire (PHQ-9). The prevalence of smoking, alcohol consumption, physical activity, sedentary lifestyle, and dietary indicators was estimated according to the presence of depression. Analyses were stratified by sex and income, and prevalence ratios were estimated using Poisson regression. Results: A significant association was found between depression and all indicators studied, except occasional alcohol consumption. Depression was associated with heavy episodic drinking and insufficient consumption of fruits and vegetables only in women. In men, the associations of depression with sedentary lifestyle and with being a former smoker were stronger than in women. Occasional alcohol consumption was more prevalent only among men without depression. In the analysis stratified by income, the association between depression and physical inactivity was stronger in the higher-income group, whereas the association with heavy episodic drinking was significant only in the lower-income stratum. Conclusion: These results highlight the need to consider mental health in programs aimed at reducing harmful health behaviors, as well as the specificities of sociodemographic groups.

### en-002
The handling and use of native species under laboratory conditions is the main obstacle to proposing species for use in bioassays. This study presents the results of rearing Paratanytarsus grimmii (Diptera: Chironomidae), a parthenogenetic species with a wide geographical distribution, under controlled conditions. Eggs were obtained from a pre-Andean river in the Araucanía region (39° S), and the conditions were determined for larval rearing with artificial food, adult handling, egg management (hatching synchronization), and collection of larvae after hatching. The life cycle of P. grimmii comprised a larval period of 23 days until the pupal stage (instars I to IV); within the following 48 hours, the adults laid eggs, which hatched after 72 hours. The following laboratory conditions were established: incubation room temperature, water pH, artificial feeding, photoperiod, and larval and egg management techniques. Because the entire life cycle could be maintained under laboratory conditions, this species can be proposed as a potential biological model for toxicity bioassays.

### en-003
In Brazil, the illegal capture of wild animals is a crime that contributes to species extinction, in addition to causing environmental imbalance and animal suffering. Here, we conducted a quantitative survey of the animals sent to the Wild Animals Triage Centers (Centros de Triagem de Animais Silvestres, CETAS) in Porto Seguro, Salvador, and Vitória da Conquista during 2009–2019. A total of 19,317, 34,460, and 43,874 specimens were recorded in the units of Porto Seguro, Vitória da Conquista, and Salvador, respectively. By class, the totals comprised 80,948 birds (82.90%), 12,007 reptiles (12.30%), 4,661 mammals (4.77%), and 35 arachnids (0.03%). The most frequent operations generating records at the CETAS were apprehensions (67,974; 69.67%), followed by voluntary surrenders (13,367; 13.69%), rescues (12,803; 13.11%), and transfers (2,735; 2.67%). The animals came from 236 municipalities in the state of Bahia, mainly Salvador, Vitória da Conquista, Feira de Santana, Lençóis, Jequié, and Paulo Afonso. The quantitative assessment of seizures by municipality indicates that the BR-116 highway is one of the most important routes for the illegal trafficking of wild animals in the state of Bahia and in the country.

### en-004
A prosocial lie is a false statement intended to help, rather than harm, someone. This study investigated the possible effects of age and culture on the responses of 97 Brazilian children (aged 7 to 11 years) in a prosocial lying task originally designed for a previous study with Canadian and Chinese children. The task consisted of four dilemmas, each followed by a question about what the child should do: tell the truth or lie to protect oneself, a friend, or a group. No effect of age was found on lying scores, but lying-for-friend scores were significantly higher than lying-for-self and lying-for-collective scores. This pattern of results suggests that, in contrast to Chinese and Canadian children, Brazilian children consider it more worthwhile to tell a lie that protects a friend than a lie that protects their own interests or those of a group.

For decades, teacher education has emphasized the importance of attending to the rapid and profound changes in society and in people's personal lives resulting from the widespread use of new digital technologies. Yet little has changed in schools, where traditional teaching still prevails. ### en-006
This study aimed to analyze the economic impact of adopting optimized, nutritionally balanced diets on Brazilian families, considering the Brazilian dietary guidelines and the economic disparities of the population. Data from the 2008–2009 Brazilian Household Budget Survey (550 strata; 55,970 households) were used. Approximately 1,700 foods and beverages purchased by Brazilians were classified into four groups according to the NOVA system. Linear programming models estimated isoenergetic diets, using the current diet as the baseline and gradually optimizing healthier diets based on the "golden rule" of the Brazilian dietary guidelines, while respecting nutritional constraints for macronutrients and micronutrients (based on international recommendations) and food acceptability limits (10th and 90th percentiles of the population's per capita calorie distribution). Diet cost was defined as the sum of the average cost of each food group in both the current and optimized diets (BRL per 2,000 kcal/person/day). The economic impact of the Brazilian dietary guidelines on household budgets was analyzed by comparing the cost of the optimized diets with that of the current diet, calculated for the total population and by income level. Three healthier diets were optimized. The current diet cost BRL 3.37 and differed between low- and high-income strata (BRL 2.62 and BRL 4.17, respectively). Regardless of income, diet cost decreased as diets approached the guidelines. However, low-income strata committed more than twice the share of their household budget committed by high-income strata (20.2% and 7.96%, respectively). Thus, the adoption of healthier eating practices can be achieved with the same or a lower budget.

### en-007
This study aimed to identify the antioxidant responses of sugarcane (Saccharum spp.) varieties subjected to water and saline stress. Sugarcane seedlings of six varieties obtained through micropropagation were subjected to water stress, saline stress, or a combination of water and saline stress. The study was carried out in May 2012 in a greenhouse at the Universidade Federal Rural de Pernambuco (UFRPE). The experimental design was completely randomized, with treatments arranged in a 6 × 4 factorial scheme (six varieties and four treatments) with four replicates. Lipid peroxidation, hydrogen peroxide (H2O2) concentration, and relative water content (RWC) were evaluated. In addition, the plants' antioxidative defense systems were assessed by measuring the activities of superoxide dismutase (SOD), ascorbate peroxidase (APX), and catalase (CAT). The sugarcane varieties showed higher lipid peroxidation and/or higher H2O2 concentrations under combined water and saline stress. The antioxidant enzymes responded differently to the water and saline stress treatments depending on the variety. Under combined water and saline stress, however, the enzymes may have become inactivated, indicating that the response to combined stress differed from the sum of the responses to water stress alone or saline stress alone. High malondialdehyde (MDA) concentrations associated with low RWC may be an effective indicator of sensitivity to multiple stresses in sugarcane varieties. The varieties RB99395 and RB867515 responded more efficiently to environmental stress and maintained their cell water content under either water or saline stress.

### en-008
Given the need to understand the variables related to the choice of Psychology specialties, this study aimed to present the adaptation process and verify validity evidence based on the internal structure of the Portuguese version of the Scale of Interests by Areas of Psychology (EIAPsi), and to relate it to personality. The sample consisted of 340 Portuguese university students who completed the EIAPsi and the NEO Five-Factor Inventory (NEO-FFI). Exploratory factor analysis and correlations between the instruments were performed. A factorial structure of 10 factors was found for the EIAPsi, with satisfactory reliability indices. The dimensions of openness and agreeableness were the most frequently correlated with the areas of Psychology. We conclude that initial validity evidence for the instrument was obtained, indicating its potential use in the Portuguese context, specifically in situations aimed at self-knowledge (in the classroom or clinic/office) and at guiding students in choosing their area of expertise.

### en-009
This study aimed to evaluate the vertical transmission of Neospora caninum in naturally infected sheep and to monitor the kinetics of antibodies against this protozoan in their lambs. Forty-eight pregnant ewes from five herds were divided into two groups: G1, seropositive for anti-N. caninum antibodies (n = 19); and G2, seronegative (n = 29). Blood samples were collected from the ewes and their lambs immediately after birth, before colostrum intake, and at 2, 7, 14, 21, 28, 35, 42, 49, and 56 days after birth. Serum antibodies were analyzed using the indirect immunofluorescence antibody test. Among the 19 seropositive ewes, six (31.6%) gave birth to lambs that were seropositive before ingesting colostrum, and these lambs remained positive until the end of the study (56 days). Only one lamb, born to a ewe with an antibody titer of 200, seroconverted after colostrum ingestion. All lambs born to seronegative ewes remained negative throughout the experimental period. We conclude that transplacental transmission was an important route of N. caninum dissemination in the herds studied and that seropositive lambs maintained circulating antibodies during the period analyzed.

### en-010
This pilot case study had two aims: to explore power and voice in small-group interactions in a secondary school classroom, and to examine the implications of researching one's own practice as a teacher-researcher. Drawing on Bakhtin's theory of dialogism, audio recordings of one small-group interaction were compared with students' independent written work to understand whose voices were used and how. Four main themes were identified: power and authority, positions of submission, silence, and the voice of the text. The findings also showed that, in the midst of teaching, we can fail to see these power and identity dynamics, whereas the time and space afforded by reflective research enable researchers to see beyond the moment and to view their teaching interventions and strategies anew, supporting the construction of new meaning.
