### tr-012
Seed coat and water content can interfere with seed tolerance to thermal shocks, as can their environment of origin. To test these possibilities, seeds of Plathymenia reticulata and Stryphnodendron adstringens were collected in northern Minas Gerais State (representing dry savanna) and northeastern Mato Grosso State (representing humid savanna) of the Cerrado biome, and their responses to thermal shocks of 110, 140, and 170 ºC for 2.5 minutes were evaluated under different conditions: intact, scarified, and imbibed after scarification. Scarified and imbibed seeds germinated faster for both species. Dry savanna seeds presented lower initial water content and slower germination for both species, but higher tolerance to thermal shocks than humid savanna seeds. P. reticulata seeds did not survive after the 140 ºC treatment, but dry savanna S. adstringens seeds endured the 170 ºC treatment, regardless of whether they were scarified and/or imbibed. It is concluded that P. reticulata seeds are less tolerant to thermal shocks than S. adstringens seeds, that dry seeds are more tolerant to thermal shocks than imbibed seeds, and that seeds from dry savannas are more tolerant to thermal shocks than seeds from humid environments.

Currently, several mortars are available for application in the construction industry. These mortars can be based on different binders, possess different physical and mechanical properties, and even other specific properties. However, selecting the correct mortar is becoming increasingly difficult. Thus, there is a need to classify different mortars based on specific criteria and a single procedure. In this way, it will be possible to identify the most appropriate mortars according to their function. In this work, a material classification procedure developed by Czarnecki and Lukowski based on a usability function was used. The classification procedure transforms experimental results of several properties into a single numerical value. In this way, construction products can be classified according to their individual properties or an optimized combination of several properties. Thus, the main objective of this study is the classification of mortars based on different binders incorporated with phase change materials (PCM). The studied binders were hydraulic lime, gypsum, and cement. For each binder, three distinct mortars were developed: a reference mortar (0% PCM), a mortar with 40% PCM incorporation, and a mortar with 40% PCM incorporation and 1% fibers. Based on the results, it was possible to conclude that the incorporation of PCM affects the overall performance of the mortars.

Background Doxorubicin is associated with cardiotoxicity and late cardiac morbidity. Heme is related to cellular oxidative stress. However, its specific regulation in cardiomyocytes under the effects of doxorubicin has not yet been documented. Objective Our objective is to evaluate changes in rate-limiting enzymes in the heme metabolic pathway under the effect of doxorubicin. Methods H9c2 cardiomyocytes were treated with doxorubicin at different concentrations (1, 2, 5, 10 μM, respectively). Real-time PCR and Western Blot assays were used to determine protein and mRNA expression for four crucial enzymes (ALAS1, ALAS2, HOX-1, and HOX-2) that regulate cellular heme metabolism, and heme levels were detected by ELISA. A p < 0.01 was considered significant. Results We observed a dose-dependent alteration pattern in heme levels in H9c2 cells, with the highest level at a doxorubicin concentration of 5 μM, which occurred synchronously with the highest level of upregulation of ALAS1 as well as the degenerative enzymes HOX-1 and HOX-2 in protein and mRNA expression. In contrast, we observed that ALAS2 was gradually downregulated, inversely proportional to doxorubicin concentrations. Conclusion Increased ALAS1 expression may play a role in elevating heme levels when H9c2 cardiomyocytes are exposed to doxorubicin and may be a therapeutic target for doxorubicin-induced myocardial toxicity. This study aimed to map intense rainfall events with durations of 10, 30, 720, and 1440 minutes, associated with return periods of 10, 50, and 100 years, for the State of Tocantins. Historical series from 95 rain gauge stations located in Tocantins State and surrounding areas were applied, covering a standardized period from 1983 to 2013. A geostatistical study was conducted, evaluating spherical, exponential, and Gaussian semivariogram models fitted by the weighted least squares method. From this study, the model presenting the lowest mean error based on cross-validation was applied to the mapping process by ordinary kriging. Geostatistical techniques performed well, highlighting the spherical model for mapping annual maximum daily rainfall, and the Gaussian model for rainfall calculated using the disaggregation methodology. It was also verified that the areas with the occurrence of the most severe intense rainfall in the state are located in Bico do Papagaio (north), Ilha do Bananal (southwest), and the northwestern region of the state.

### tr-017
Abdominal aortic aneurysm rupture is an event with high mortality, and its treatment in these cases is a medical emergency. Endovascular treatment of these aneurysms has established itself as a minimally invasive alternative to classic open surgery, becoming the first-choice option. However, 20% to 50% of patients with abdominal aortic aneurysm do not present favorable anatomy for endovascular treatment due to the presence of a short neck or involvement of visceral branches by the aneurysm. We report the case of a 70-year-old female patient who underwent endovascular repair of a ruptured juxtarenal aneurysm with parallel stent implantation to the renal arteries (chimney technique). Clinical data and procedural details are presented. Technical success was achieved, and no postoperative complications were reported.

### tr-018
The objective of this work was to test the viability of using the vaginal route in female cattle for oxytocin administration using specific formulations. Oxytocin was used associated with absorption enhancers and in injectable form (for comparison). Seven formulations were produced, all with a concentration of 5 IU/mL of oxytocin. The experimental design was an 8x8 Latin square (eight days, eight treatments, and eight cycling heifers). Oxytocin administrations were via the vaginal route, and injectable oxytocin was administered intravenously, each containing 10 IU. Blood samples (n=7) were collected from each animal at time 0, prior to treatment administration, and every three minutes up to 18 minutes. Oxytocin was extracted from plasma using a C-18 chromatographic column and quantified using the commercial Oxytocin EIA kit. The obtained results were evaluated by non-parametric statistical analysis using the Kruskal-Wallis test for mean comparisons. Oxytocin concentration differed between time 0 and time 12 minutes (p<0.05) for the oxytocin in glycerin formulation. It was concluded that absorption occurred through the vaginal route for this formulation. Further studies will be necessary for its effective use in cattle management.
