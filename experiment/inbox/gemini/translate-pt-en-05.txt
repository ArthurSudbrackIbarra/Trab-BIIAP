### tr-042
Although Araliaceae includes many species with leaf margins featuring serrated teeth, the anatomical structure of these projections has never been investigated. Thus, this study aimed to characterize the leaf tooth anatomy of Hydrocotyle asterias Cham. & Schltdl., a representative of this family. Leaf blade fragments containing marginal teeth were processed using standard light microscopy techniques (diaphanization, paradermal cuts, and histochemical tests) and scanning electron microscopy. The teeth of H. asterias consist of hydathodes, whose size, shape, and interdental spacing vary across different stages of leaf development. They exhibit a unistratified epidermis composed of isodiametric cells with slightly thickened walls, a thin cuticle, and stomata concentrated in the apical region. The vascularization consists of xylem projecting into the epithem. This study provides the first information on the anatomical structure of leaf teeth in the Araliaceae family and expands knowledge about hydathodes located on leaf teeth.

The objective of this study was to estimate the potential for soil loss using the Universal Soil Loss Equation (USLE) and to identify the factors controlling the erosive process in a hillslope watershed. The rainfall erosivity factor (R) was calculated using normal precipitation data, yielding monthly erosion indices. The soil erodibility factor (K) was obtained from soil sampling conducted across a 340-point grid, with values interpolated using ordinary kriging. The topographic factor (LS) was generated from a Digital Elevation Model (DEM), whereas the cover-management and support practice factors (CP) were obtained through field observations and tabulated values. The LS factor determined the magnitude of the erosive process, while the CP factor showed the highest correlation with soil loss control. ### tr-045
This study presents an application of the WRF mesoscale model to simulate an episode of intense and persistent wind that occurs with irregular frequency over the Guapé reservoir, Minas Gerais. Such events disrupt the local population's daily routine, causing, for example, the temporary suspension of the ferry service operating between the cities of Guapé and Capitólio. During one of these episodes, which occurred on September 22, 2016, measurements were taken using a Zephir ZP300 LIDAR and an anemometric tower; these data were subsequently used to validate the simulations. To evaluate the results, simulated and observed values of pressure, temperature, humidity, and wind speed were compared. The analysis of simulated vertical velocity profiles suggests the presence of a low-level jet located approximately 500 m above the surface, generated by a post-frontal temperature gradient, whose lower portion was accelerated by the topography and land-water surface roughness variation. Further studies are currently underway to propose preventive measures to the local population and authorities.

### tr-047
Bacterial pustule, caused by the bacterium Xanthomonas axonopodis pv. glycines, is considered one of the most severe bacterial diseases affecting soybean crops. The objective of this work was to develop and validate a diagrammatic scale for assessing the severity of bacterial pustule in soybean. To construct the scale, soybean leaflets showing varying levels of severity were collected to establish the minimum and maximum disease severity values, while intermediate severity levels were set according to the Weber-Fechner law of visual perception. The proposed values for the scale were: 1.4%, 3%, 6%, 9%, 20%, and 29.5%. To validate the scale, eight raters with no prior experience in disease assessment evaluated 39 photographs showing different severity levels, both with and without the aid of the scale. The severity values estimated by the raters were compared to the actual severity values (determined using Assess 2.0 software from digital images of symptomatic leaves) and subjected to simple linear regressions to evaluate rater accuracy. Rater precision was determined using coefficients of determination and the variance of absolute errors. Estimates made using the proposed diagrammatic scale showed a significant increase in the coefficient of determination and lower variance of absolute errors, providing greater precision and accuracy in evaluations, thus confirming its suitability for assessing bacterial pustule severity in soybean.

For this purpose, 160 seed bank samples were collected: 80 from an MOF remnant and 80 from a P. taeda stand. The samples were germinated in two separate greenhouses under 35% and 75% shading levels. A total of 11,669 woody and herbaceous individuals were recorded, distributed across 31 botanical families and 84 species. The Asteraceae and Poaceae families were the most representative in terms of herbaceous species richness. Among woody species, Solanaceae and Asteraceae were the most representative in both areas. The species P. taeda was recorded exclusively in the seed bank of its own stand. The seed bank of the pine stand was mainly characterized by low woody species richness, demonstrating its influence on the seed stock. Conversely, the evaluated MOF remnant does not appear to be influenced by the proximity of the pine stand regarding its seed bank.
