### tr-061
ASTM A572 Grade 50 steel is used in the manufacture of welded assemblies for wind turbines; for this reason, performing stress relief is of great importance to correct the impacts caused by welding processes, as well as to understand the final properties obtained through this process. The objective of this study is to compare the mechanical properties and residual stresses (blind hole method) in ASTM A572 Grade 50 steel samples welded by the submerged arc welding process, subjected to stress relief in the heat-treated condition (PWHT) and in the as-welded condition (SST), following the normative requirements of AWS D1.1-2015. The results for the PWHT condition regarding hardness, yield strength, tensile strength, and residual stresses are lower, while elongation and impact absorbed energy values are higher than in the SST condition. Both conditions passed the side bend test. The obtained results demonstrate the importance of the stress relief process, which minimizes the undesirable effects of welding processes on components.

### tr-062
The objective of this study was to verify the influence of supplementation with different combinations of organic minerals on performance and carcass characteristics, as well as to evaluate their economic viability, for finishing pigs. Eighty-eight barrows were used, divided among four treatments: control with basal diet, and supplementation with CrFe, MgSe, and CrFeMgSe during the 28 days prior to slaughter. The experimental design was randomized blocks, with the experimental unit represented by two animals. Animals supplemented with CrFeMgSe exhibited worse final weight, daily weight gain, and feed intake across the entire period, with a consequent reduction in carcass weight, smaller loin depth, ribeye area, and lower amount of lean meat in the carcass. There was an increase in diet cost for the CrFe, MgSe, and CrFeMgSe groups, respectively. The use of chromium plus iron and magnesium plus selenium, combined two by two, does not alter performance parameters or carcass characteristics. However, the association of the four minerals impairs these characteristics and increases diet cost.

Unfavorable meteorological conditions for pollutant dispersion, combined with high atmospheric pollutant emission rates and complex topography, frequently favor high pollutant concentrations in the Metropolitan Region of Rio de Janeiro (RMRJ). ### tr-064
Introduction: The identification of primary palatoplasty complications may vary among professionals from different health fields due to the lack of standardization in fistula classification. This study aimed to verify the consensus between plastic surgery (PS) and speech-language pathology (SLP) professionals regarding the occurrence of fistula, as reported in the same craniofacial service. Methods: An analysis of PS and SLP records regarding the presence and location of fistulas was conducted, as reported in 466 medical charts of patients with a history of unilateral incisive foramen cleft. To compare the findings between both fields, a gold-standard verification of fistula occurrence (GSO) was established by an experienced plastic surgeon. Results: The PS area reported that 25% of the 466 patients presented a fistula, compared to 37% reported by SLP, while the GSO indicated a fistula in 35% of cases. Kappa statistics revealed fair agreement between GSO and PS (r=0.32) and substantial agreement between GSO and SLP (r=0.63). Conclusion: Disagreement was observed between speech-language pathology and plastic surgery regarding the occurrence and location of fistulas following primary palatoplasties in the same craniofacial center. The data point to the need for creating and implementing a standardized fistula classification system that can be effectively used by craniofacial teams, promoting scientific evidence regarding cleft lip and palate treatment outcomes.

### tr-066
Cassava black rot, caused by Scytalidium lignicola, has become an important disease for producing states due to major yield losses. Therefore, the objective of this study was to evaluate the effect of sources and doses of organic matter, isolated and added to sandy soil, on the suppressiveness of the mycelial growth of S. lignicola CMM 1098. Two experiments were conducted: the first evaluated the effect of organic material extracts (poultry litter, goat manure, cattle manure, and earthworm humus) incorporated into potato dextrose agar (PDA) medium at concentrations of 10, 20, 30, and 40% (v/v); the second evaluated the effect of organic materials mixed with sandy soil at the same sources and doses. Poultry litter was the most efficient source in inhibiting the mycelial growth of S. lignicola at a 40% dose, achieving a mycelial growth inhibition of 69.75% only when the extracts were unautoclaved. Unautoclaved poultry litter at doses equal to or greater than 30% was effective in suppressing the mycelial growth of S. lignicola CMM 1098. Unautoclaved sandy soil combined with sources and doses of organic matter is highly effective in suppressing the growth of S. lignicola CMM 1098.

### tr-067
In this study, the microstructure and intergranular corrosion resistance of ferritic stainless steels with Nb, Ti, and (Ti+Nb) additions subjected to TIG welding were investigated. Structural characterization of the weld beads was performed using optical microscopy (OM), scanning electron microscopy (SEM), and energy-dispersive X-ray spectroscopy (EDS). Sensitization susceptibility was evaluated qualitatively using Practice W of the ASTM A763 standard and quantitatively using the DL-EPR technique. A martensite network was observed at the grain boundaries in the weld region of the unstabilized steel, whereas a completely ferritic microstructure was observed in the steel samples with stabilizer additions. Practice W revealed general corrosion inside the grains of AISI 430 steel and the presence of ditches in AISI 430 Ti; concomitantly, the DL-EPR technique confirmed that these steels were sensitized by welding. In contrast, samples of steels with Nb and (Ti+Nb) additions maintained good corrosion resistance, remaining free from sensitization after welding.

In critical situations resulting from low water levels in hydroelectric reservoirs, the control of the Brazilian electricity sector is carried out based on hydrothermal planning that depends on future inflow discharges to Hydroelectric Power Plants (HPPs). Two computational models are used to optimize the system: NEWAVE (medium and long term) and DECOMP (short term). NEWAVE is an energy planning model applied to the National Interconnected System (SIN) that uses Stochastic Dual Dynamic Programming (SDDP) to determine the operational policy that minimizes the marginal operating cost over a horizon of up to 5 years. The objective of this work is to evaluate the performance of NEWAVE through the NWLISTOP functionality in projecting Natural Inflow Energy (ENA) and Stored Energy (EAR) in the Northeast (NE) Subsystem for the years 2012 to 2016. The results indicate the need to use techniques that enable optimizing long-term operation planning, especially during extreme drought episodes.

The Açu National Forest (FLONA) is located in a section of the border of the Potiguar Basin with pebble deposits that make the soil stony. The objective of this work was to characterize the phytosociological structure of the Caatinga within and around the FLONA and to understand the biogeographical links of the local flora relative to other areas of the Caatinga. We selected areas inside and around the FLONA and sampled 1,500 woody individuals belonging to 33 species. The biogeographical links show that the stony border of the Potiguar Basin shares greater affinity with the flora of crystalline terrains than with the sandy terrains of other sandy sedimentary basins.
