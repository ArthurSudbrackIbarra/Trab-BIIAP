### en-041
Objective: This study evaluated heavy metal concentrations in sweet potato (*Ipomoea batatas*) and spinach (*Spinacia oleracea*) associated with unmanaged municipal waste, vehicle emissions, pesticides, and fertilizers in the Urban West Region of Zanzibar. Methods: Twenty samples were collected at random from local markets and three roadside farms in the Urban West Region. Samples were analyzed via energy-dispersive X-ray fluorescence at the Tanzania Atomic Energy Commission in Arusha. Results: Essential and toxic elements (Mn, Cu, Fe, As, Zn, Pb, and Ni) were detected in both crops. *I. batatas* accumulated higher concentrations of Cr, Fe, Cd, and Zn, whereas spinach exhibited higher levels of Ni, Cr, and Zn. Essential element concentrations in both crops fell within safe baseline limits for human consumption. However, toxic element concentrations exceeded FAO/WHO permissible limits. High levels of toxic metals in these crops are likely linked to contamination from municipal waste, poultry manure, vehicle exhaust, or pesticides. Conclusion: Cultivating vegetables along roadsides and applying untreated poultry manure and fertilizers in the Urban West Region of Zanzibar introduces elevated levels of heavy metals into the food chain.

### en-042
Objective: This study investigated the genetic diversity of 'Zuijinxiang' grape (*Vitis vinifera*) and its mutated $F_1$ offspring to screen for superior mutant lines with breeding potential. Methods: Fifty mutant single plants derived from ${}^{137}\text{Cs}$-$\gamma$-irradiated 'Zuijinxiang' grape seeds were evaluated. Start Codon Target (SCoT) molecular markers were used to analyze genetic diversity, variation, and cluster relationships. Results: (1) Thirty-six SCoT primers produced clear, reproducible, and highly polymorphic bands, achieving 100% amplification efficiency and polymorphism rates. (2) The 36 primers amplified 221 total bands, 175 of which were polymorphic (average polymorphism rate: 80.3%; mean genetic similarity coefficient: 0.916). (3) The number of observed alleles ($N_a$) ranged from 4 to 8 (mean: 6.1389); effective alleles ($N_e$) ranged from 1.2772 to 5.6322 (mean: 3.5968); expected heterozygosity ($H_e$) ranged from 0.2192 to 0.8344 (mean: 0.6965); observed heterozygosity ($H_o$) ranged from 0.1656 to 0.7808 (mean: 0.3035); Nei's gene diversity index ($H$) ranged from 0.2170 to 0.8224 (mean: 0.6863); and the Shannon-Wiener index ($I$) ranged from 0.5186 to 1.8597 (mean: 1.4517). (4) UPGMA cluster analysis divided the 51 accessions into three major groups at a genetic distance threshold of 0.856. Conclusion: Radiation-induced mutational germplasm of 'Zuijinxiang' grape possesses rich genetic diversity. Furthermore, SCoT molecular markers successfully discriminate closely related mutant lines, providing a reliable method for early identification and development of elite 'Zuijinxiang' grape mutants.

### en-043
Silicon (Si) and proline contribute significantly to plant physiological and metabolic functioning under water deficit, enhancing water-use efficiency and antioxidant activity. This study evaluated the capacity of Si to mitigate water deficit stress in cowpea (*Vigna unguiculata*) cultivars through physiological, biochemical, and growth metrics. A randomized block design with a $4 \times 4$ factorial scheme and five replications was used, combining four cultivars (BRS Guariba, BRS Itaim, BRS Aracê, and BRS Rouxinol) and four irrigation treatments with or without Si ($W_{100}$: 100% $\text{ET}_o$; $W_{50}$: 50% $\text{ET}_o$; $W_{50}+100\text{Si}$ and $W_{50}+200\text{Si}$, containing 100 and 200 mg L$^{-1}$ Si, respectively). Water deficit reduced leaf water potential across all cultivars, impairing plant growth. However, Si applications of 200 mg L$^{-1}$ in 'BRS Guariba' and 100 or 200 mg L$^{-1}$ in 'BRS Itaim' attenuated stress by increasing leaf water potential and ascorbate peroxidase activity, while increasing proline concentration in 'BRS Guariba' and decreasing it in 'BRS Itaim', maintaining vegetative growth. In 'BRS Rouxinol' and 'BRS Aracê', Si applications at 100 and 200 mg L$^{-1}$, respectively, reduced stress-induced growth suppression by modulating enzymatic metabolism and proline levels, despite not increasing leaf water potential.

Recent research demonstrates that modified formulations—the Modified Effective Stress Approach (MESA) and Modified Total Stress Approach (MTSA)—can interpret and predict the bearing capacity of unsaturated soils by incorporating matric suction effects. However, comprehensive reviews detailing practical geotechnical applications of MESA and MTSA remain limited. This state-of-the-art review outlines the theoretical foundations of MESA and MTSA and evaluates existing analytical and numerical models for unsaturated soil bearing capacity. Available methodologies are classified into MESA and MTSA framework categories and illustrated through practical application examples. This paper provides theoretical insights and predictive tools to support the integration of unsaturated soil mechanics into geotechnical engineering practice.

### en-048
This study assessed maternal and paternal conceptualizations of six emotions (joy, sadness, anger, fear, pride, and shame), parents' beliefs regarding the developmental appropriateness of emotional expression in young children, and associations with parental self-construal profiles (autonomy, relatedness, and autonomous-relatedness). ### en-050
Thermotherapy of sugarcane setts is the primary management strategy for *Leifsonia xyli* subsp. *xyli* (Lxx) in Brazil, relying on thermal denaturation of bacterial proteins without damaging vegetative buds. However, incomplete pathogen eradication can cause asymptomatic infections in propagated plants. This study evaluated molecular detection of Lxx in sugarcane setts subjected to various temperature-time combinations, with or without antibiotic supplementation. Standard PCR detected Lxx DNA only in positive controls exhibiting high bacterial titers. In contrast, nested PCR detected Lxx DNA across all thermotherapy treatments. None of the tested heat treatments completely eliminated Lxx from the setts. Kasugamycin supplementation reduced bacterial loads but failed to achieve complete eradication. These results demonstrate that thermotherapy alone does not clear Lxx infections and confirm that nested PCR is an essential diagnostic tool for screening seed cane.
