This demonstrates that potential conversions from one land cover type to another may influence the surface radiation balance. In other words, if oil palm cultivation continues to expand over pastureland, surface albedo would decrease, consequently leading to changes in the microclimate. However, if expansion occurs over areas of secondary vegetation or forest, the opposite would occur, potentially contributing to climate change.
