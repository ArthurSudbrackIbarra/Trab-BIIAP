Guava rootstocks can be propagated using minicuttings under high relative humidity, although an acclimatization period is required. Certain growth regulators can improve acclimatization, and brassinosteroids have been successfully used in other fruit crops. This study aimed to reduce the production time of the BRS Guaraçá hybrid by applying Biobras-16 (BB-16) during the acclimatization of seedlings obtained from minicuttings. Five BB-16 concentrations (0, 0.1, 0.3, 0.5, and 1.0 mg L-1) were evaluated in a randomized block design with a split-plot arrangement, four replications, and five seedlings per plot. The experiment was conducted in two seasons: winter and spring/summer. After 60 days of acclimatization, shoot and root growth indicators increased in response to BB-16 application, particularly at concentrations between 0.3 and 0.6 mg L-1. These results indicate that brassinosteroids can be used to stimulate the growth of BRS Guaraçá seedlings propagated from minicuttings, thereby shortening production time and improving seedling quality.

**Objective:** This review aimed to summarize the current evidence on the use of biological agents for CD and UC in Latin America.

**Methods:** Data were drawn from a previously conducted comprehensive systematic review investigating various characteristics of inflammatory bowel disease (IBD) in Latin America. Studies included in that review were selected for the present analysis if they provided detailed information on the proportion of patients receiving biological agents in cohorts from Latin American and Caribbean countries. Descriptive findings on the use of these drugs in CD and UC were compiled across patient cohorts and countries.

**Results:** Of the 61 studies included in the original systematic review, only 19 reported the proportion of patients treated with biological agents. **Conclusion:** The use of anti-TNF agents in Latin America is comparable to that reported in other regions for CD but remains lower for UC. The study population comprised non-financial companies listed on the São Paulo Stock, Commodities, and Futures Exchange (BM&FBovespa). The sample included 247 firms per year over the period from 2000 to 2015, totaling 2,501 observations. Discretionary accruals were estimated using the Pae (2005) and Paulo (2007) models. Real earnings management was estimated following Roychowdhury (2006), considering only abnormal production costs and operational decisions. The results show that earnings management strategies, whether based on accruals or real manipulation, as well as the choice between these strategies, are influenced by the economic environment. The evidence indicates that managers exhibit different opportunistic behaviors across business cycle phases. Specifically, they increase discretionary accruals during contractionary phases and reduce them during recoveries, while managing earnings downward through real activities manipulation during recessions and contractions.
