### tr-061
ASTM A572 Grade 50 steel is used in the manufacture of welded assemblies for wind turbines. Therefore, stress relief is essential for correcting the effects caused by welding processes, as well as for understanding the final properties achieved through this process. This study aims to compare the mechanical properties and residual stresses (measured using the hole-drilling method) of ASTM A572 Grade 50 steel samples welded by the submerged arc welding process and subjected either to post-weld heat treatment (PWHT) or tested in the as-welded condition (SST), in accordance with the requirements of AWS D1.1-2015. Compared with the SST condition, the PWHT condition resulted in lower hardness, yield strength, tensile strength, and residual stress values, and higher elongation and impact energy absorption values. Both conditions passed the side-bend test. The results demonstrate the importance of the stress-relief process in minimizing the undesirable effects of welding on components.

### tr-062
This study aimed to investigate the effects of supplementation with different combinations of organic minerals on performance and carcass characteristics, as well as to evaluate its economic feasibility in finishing pigs. A total of 88 castrated males were assigned to four treatments: a control group receiving a basal diet and three supplemented groups receiving CrFe, MgSe, and CrFeMgSe, respectively, during the 28 days preceding slaughter. The experimental design was a randomized block design, with each experimental unit consisting of two animals. Animals supplemented with CrFeMgSe showed poorer final weight, daily weight gain, and feed intake throughout the experimental period, resulting in lower carcass weight, reduced loin depth and loin eye area, and a lower amount of lean meat in the carcass. Diet costs increased for the CrFe, MgSe, and CrFeMgSe groups, respectively. The combined use of chromium and iron and magnesium and selenium, in pairs, did not affect performance parameters or carcass characteristics. However, the combination of all four minerals negatively affected these characteristics and increased diet costs.

### tr-063
Unfavorable meteorological conditions for pollutant dispersion, combined with high atmospheric pollutant emission rates and complex topography, often lead to high pollutant concentrations in the Metropolitan Region of Rio de Janeiro (MRRJ). In this context, this study investigated the influence of meteorological conditions during two periods in which high concentrations of O3 and PM10 occurred on consecutive days in the MRRJ. The study used the Weather Research and Forecasting (WRF) mesoscale numerical model to simulate the meteorological conditions and variables favorable to the persistence of these episodes, examining the influence of the South Atlantic Subtropical High (SASH), high air temperatures, low wind speeds, and the absence of cloud cover, in agreement with observational data. Visual analysis showed that the model effectively represented the SASH, precipitation, and cloud cover. The model yielded positive Pearson linear correlation coefficients above 0.71 between simulated and observed temperatures in both periods at all eight weather stations, and positive Pearson linear correlation coefficients above 0.72 between simulated and observed relative humidity. The calculation of mean error (ME) indicated that, in general, the model underestimated air temperature and overestimated relative humidity. The application of statistical methods to the observed and model-simulated data revealed that the model had skill in representing meteorological variables at most stations.

### tr-064
**Introduction:** The identification of complications following primary palatoplasty may vary among professionals from different healthcare fields due to the lack of a standardized fistula classification system. This study aimed to assess the agreement between plastic surgery (PS) and speech-language pathology (SLP) professionals regarding the occurrence of fistulas, as reported within the same craniofacial service.

**Methods:** Records from the PS and SLP departments were analyzed for the presence and location of fistulas, as reported in 466 medical records of patients with a history of unilateral complete cleft lip and palate. To compare the findings from both departments, an experienced plastic surgeon established a gold-standard assessment of fistula occurrence (GSF).

**Results:** The PS department reported fistulas in 25% of the 466 patients, compared with 37% reported by the SLP department, while the GSF indicated fistulas in 35% of cases. Kappa statistics revealed fair agreement between the GSF and PS (r = 0.32) and substantial agreement between the GSF and SLP (r = 0.63).

**Conclusion:** Disagreement was observed between the speech-language pathology and plastic surgery departments regarding the occurrence and location of fistulas following primary palatoplasty within the same craniofacial center. These findings highlight the need to develop and implement a standardized fistula classification system that can be effectively used by craniofacial teams, thereby supporting the generation of scientific evidence on the outcomes of cleft lip and palate treatment.

Black rot of cassava, caused by Scytalidium lignicola, has become an important disease in cassava-producing states due to the substantial production losses it causes. Therefore, this study aimed to evaluate the effects of different sources and doses of organic matter, applied alone or mixed with sandy soil, on the suppression of mycelial growth of S. lignicola CMM 1098. Two experiments were conducted. The first evaluated the effects of extracts from organic materials (poultry litter, goat manure, cattle manure, and earthworm castings) incorporated into potato dextrose agar (PDA) culture medium at concentrations of 10%, 20%, 30%, and 40% (v/v). The second evaluated the effects of the same organic materials mixed with sandy soil at the same sources and application rates. Poultry litter was the most effective source for inhibiting the mycelial growth of S. lignicola at a 40% rate, achieving 69.75% inhibition only when the extracts were not autoclaved. Non-autoclaved poultry litter at rates of 30% or higher effectively suppressed the mycelial growth of S. lignicola CMM 1098. Non-autoclaved sandy soil amended with the different sources and rates of organic matter was highly effective in suppressing the growth of S. lignicola CMM 1098.

### tr-067
This study investigated the microstructure and intergranular corrosion resistance of ferritic stainless steels containing Nb, Ti, and (Ti+Nb) additions and subjected to gas tungsten arc welding (GTAW/TIG). The weld bead structure was characterized using optical microscopy (OM), scanning electron microscopy (SEM), and energy-dispersive X-ray spectroscopy (EDS). Susceptibility to sensitization was qualitatively assessed using Practice W of ASTM A763 and quantitatively evaluated using the double-loop electrochemical potentiokinetic reactivation (DL-EPR) technique. A martensitic network was observed along the grain boundaries in the weld region of the non-stabilized steel, whereas the samples of steels containing stabilizing additions exhibited a fully ferritic microstructure. Practice W revealed generalized corrosion within the grains of AISI 430 steel and the presence of ditches in AISI 430 Ti. In conjunction with the DL-EPR technique, these findings confirmed that these steels had been sensitized by welding. In contrast, the samples of steels containing Nb and (Ti+Nb) additions retained good corrosion resistance and remained free from sensitization after welding.

### tr-068
This study aims to analyze Primary Health Care (PHC) as a learning environment for medical students. A descriptive cross-sectional study with a qualitative approach was conducted through four focus groups involving medical interns from the four medical schools in Fortaleza, Ceará, Brazil. The data were analyzed using the interpretation-of-meanings method, and two empirical categories were identified. According to the students, Primary Health Care is an important teaching setting for connecting theory and practice, but they do not fully recognize its importance within the Brazilian Unified Health System (SUS). Consequently, they do not regard defending the SUS as an important professional principle. However, encouragement to pursue teaching in Primary Health Care and the development of humanistic skills during medical training were identified as factors that could motivate students to work in this field.

Two computational models are used to optimize the system: NEWAVE (medium- and long-term planning) and DECOMP (short-term planning). NEWAVE is an energy planning model applied to the National Interconnected System (SIN) that uses Stochastic Dual Dynamic Programming (SDDP) to determine the operating policy that minimizes the marginal operating cost over a planning horizon of up to five years. The results indicate the need to use techniques that improve long-term operational planning, particularly during extreme drought episodes.

Açu National Forest is located along a section of the Potiguar Basin margin characterized by pebble deposits, which make the soil stony. This study aimed to characterize the phytosociological structure of the Caatinga vegetation within the National Forest and its surroundings and to understand the biogeographic relationships between the local flora and that of other Caatinga areas. We selected sites within and around the National Forest and sampled 1,500 woody individuals belonging to 33 species. The biogeographic relationships indicate that the stony margin of the Potiguar Basin is more closely related to the flora of crystalline terrains than to that of sandy terrains in other sandy sedimentary basins.
