The teeth of H. asterias consist of hydathodes, whose size, shape, and spacing vary across the different stages of leaf development. Bacterial pustule, caused by the bacterium Xanthomonas axonopodis pv. glycines, is considered one of the most serious bacterial diseases affecting soybean crops. The aim of this study was to develop and validate a diagrammatic scale for assessing the severity of bacterial pustule in soybean. To develop the scale, soybean leaflets with different severity levels were collected to determine the minimum and maximum disease severity values. The intermediate severity levels on the scale were established according to the Weber-Fechner law of visual stimulus. The proposed scale values were 1.4%, 3%, 6%, 9%, 20%, and 29.5%. To validate the scale, eight evaluators with no prior experience in assessing the disease examined 39 photographs showing different severity levels, both with and without the aid of the scale. The severity estimates made by the evaluators were compared with the actual severity values, which had been determined using Assess 2.0 software based on digital images of symptomatic leaves, and subjected to simple linear regression analyses to assess evaluator accuracy. Evaluator precision was determined using the coefficients of determination and the variance of absolute errors. Estimates obtained with the proposed diagrammatic scale showed a significant increase in the coefficient of determination and a lower variance of absolute errors, resulting in greater precision and accuracy in the assessments. Therefore, the scale can be adopted to assess bacterial pustule severity in soybean.
