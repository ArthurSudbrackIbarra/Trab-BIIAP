Objective: To analyze the association between depression and various health behaviors and determine whether these associations differ by sex or income level. Methods: This cross-sectional study used data from 65,803 Brazilian adults aged 18–59 years who participated in the 2019 National Health Survey. Depression status was assessed using the Patient Health Questionnaire-9 (PHQ-9). The prevalence of smoking, alcohol consumption, physical activity, sedentary behavior, and dietary indicators was estimated according to depression status. Analyses were stratified by sex and income level, and prevalence ratios were estimated using Poisson regression. Results: Significant associations were observed between depression and all indicators studied, except occasional alcohol consumption. Depression was associated with heavy episodic drinking and insufficient fruit and vegetable consumption only among women. Among men, the associations of depression with sedentary behavior and former smoking were stronger than those observed among women. Occasional alcohol consumption was more prevalent only among men without depression. Income-stratified analyses showed that the association between depression and physical inactivity was stronger in the higher-income group, whereas the association with heavy episodic drinking was significant only in the lower-income group. Conclusion: These findings highlight the need to consider mental health in programs aimed at reducing harmful health behaviors while accounting for the specific characteristics of different sociodemographic groups.

The management and maintenance of native species under laboratory conditions are major challenges when selecting species for use in bioassays. This study presents the results of the successful maintenance of Paratanytarsus grimmii (Diptera: Chironomidae), a parthenogenetic species with a wide geographical distribution, under controlled laboratory conditions. Specimens were collected from a pre-Andean river in the Araucanía region (39° S). The study established conditions for larval rearing using artificial food, adult emergence and handling, egg collection and hatching synchronization, and larval rearing following hatching. The life cycle of P. grimmii included a larval period of 23 days, from the first to the fourth instar, followed by pupation. Adults emerged within the subsequent 48 hours and deposited eggs, which hatched after 72 hours. Laboratory conditions were established, including incubation room temperature, water pH, artificial feeding, photoperiod, and techniques for handling larvae and eggs. Based on these results, the complete life cycle of P. grimmii was successfully maintained under laboratory conditions, supporting its potential use as biological material in toxicity bioassays.

### en-004
A prosocial lie is a false statement intended to benefit another person rather than cause harm. This study investigated the potential effects of age and culture on the responses of 97 Brazilian children aged 7–11 years in a prosocial lying task previously developed for a study involving Canadian and Chinese children. The task consisted of four dilemmas, each followed by questions about whether children should tell the truth or lie to protect themselves, a friend, or a group. No age-related effect was observed on lying scores. However, scores for lying to protect a friend were significantly higher than those for lying to protect oneself or a collective interest. This pattern suggests that, unlike their Canadian and Chinese counterparts, Brazilian children consider lying to protect a friend more worthwhile than lying to protect their own interests or those of a group.

This study aimed to analyze the economic impact of adopting optimized, nutritionally balanced diets among Brazilian households, taking into account the Brazilian Dietary Guidelines and the population's socioeconomic disparities. Data from the 2008–2009 Brazilian Household Budget Survey, comprising 550 strata and 55,970 households, were used. Approximately 1,700 foods and beverages purchased by Brazilian households were classified into four groups according to the NOVA classification system. Linear programming models were used to estimate isoenergetic diets, preserving the current diet as the baseline and progressively optimizing healthier diets based on the "golden rule" of the Brazilian Dietary Guidelines. Nutritional constraints for macronutrients and micronutrients, based on international recommendations, and food acceptability limits, defined by the 10th and 90th percentiles of the population's per capita calorie distribution, were considered. Diet costs were calculated by summing the average costs of each food group in both the current and optimized diets, expressed in BRL per 2,000 kcal/person/day. The economic impact of adopting the Brazilian Dietary Guidelines on household budgets was assessed by comparing the costs of optimized diets with those of the current diet for the total population and across income groups. Three healthier diets were optimized. The cost of the current diet was BRL 3.37, with differences between the low- and high-income groups (BRL 2.62 and BRL 4.17, respectively). Regardless of income level, diet costs decreased as diets moved closer to the recommendations. However, the proportion of household income allocated to food was more than twice as high among low-income households as among high-income households (20.2% and 7.96%, respectively). These findings indicate that healthier dietary practices can be adopted without increasing, and potentially while reducing, food expenditures.

### en-007
This study aimed to investigate the antioxidant responses of different sugarcane (Saccharum spp.) varieties subjected to water and saline stress. Seedlings of six sugarcane varieties obtained through micropropagation were exposed to water stress, saline stress, or a combination of both. The experiment was conducted in May 2012 in a greenhouse at the Federal Rural University of Pernambuco (UFRPE). A randomized experimental design was employed, with treatments arranged in a 6 × 4 factorial scheme comprising six varieties and four treatments, with four replicates. Lipid peroxidation, hydrogen peroxide (H₂O₂) concentrations, and relative water content (RWC) were assessed. The antioxidant defense system was also evaluated by measuring the activities of superoxide dismutase (SOD), ascorbate peroxidase (APX), and catalase (CAT). Sugarcane varieties exhibited increased lipid peroxidation and/or higher H₂O₂ concentrations under combined water and saline stress. The antioxidant enzymes responded differently to water and saline stress depending on the variety. However, under combined stress conditions, these enzymes may have become inactivated, indicating that the response to simultaneous water and saline stress differed from the sum of the responses to each stress applied individually. High malondialdehyde (MDA) concentrations associated with low RWC may serve as effective indicators of susceptibility to multiple stresses in sugarcane varieties. The RB99395 and RB867515 varieties exhibited more efficient responses to environmental stress and maintained their cellular water content when exposed to either water or saline stress.

### en-009
This study aimed to evaluate the vertical transmission of Neospora caninum in naturally infected sheep and monitor the kinetics of antibodies against this protozoan in their lambs. A total of 48 pregnant ewes from five herds were divided into two groups: G1, consisting of 19 animals seropositive for anti-N. caninum antibodies, and G2, consisting of 29 seronegative animals. Blood samples were collected from the ewes and their lambs immediately after birth, before colostrum ingestion, and at 2, 7, 14, 21, 28, 35, 42, 49, and 56 days after birth. Serum antibody levels were assessed using the indirect fluorescent antibody test. Among the 19 seropositive ewes, six (31.6%) gave birth to lambs that were seropositive before colostrum ingestion. These lambs remained seropositive throughout the study period, up to 56 days of age. Only one lamb, born to an ewe with an antibody titer of 200, seroconverted after colostrum ingestion. All lambs born to seronegative ewes remained seronegative throughout the experimental period. These findings indicate that transplacental transmission is an important route of N. caninum dissemination within the herds studied and that seropositive lambs maintain circulating antibodies throughout the period analyzed.
