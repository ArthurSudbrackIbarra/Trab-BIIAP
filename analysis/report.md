## Lower bound on the share of abstracts processed by an LLM

Cross-fitted: the marker set (style excess words below the threshold T that maximizes the gap) is chosen on one random half of the abstracts and measured on the other, both ways. Standard errors come from 1000 resamples of the journals, with the marker sets held fixed; the binomial standard error, which treats abstracts as independent, is shown for comparison. Tighter bound: gap / (1 - expected), valid under the same assumptions because no more than every processed abstract can carry a marker. Panel: journals and abstracts of the year (both halves). Same data: chosen and measured on all abstracts, as Kobak et al. did. Placebo: the same-data markers with the same extrapolation span, from 2015 and 2016.
